# Transfer case fluid change (TX15B)

[Guides](index.md) · [Reference home](../index.md) · [Fluids and capacities](../fluids-capacities.md)

| | |
|---|---|
| Applies to | 2005-2015 4WD Xterra. Every 4WD truck has the TX15B part-time transfer case (2WD, 4H, 4LO) [S1][S2][S3] |
| Difficulty | Easy, about 30 minutes [Unverified] |
| Interval | On Nissan's severe-use Schedule 1, inspect every 15,000 mi (24,000 km). If you tow, carry a roof load, or drive rough or muddy roads, change it every 30,000 mi (48,000 km) or 24 months [S1] |

## Specifications

| Item | Value | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Fluid | Genuine Nissan Matic D ATF (the 2006 manual also allows DEXRON III or MERCON) | [S1][S3] |
| Capacity (approximate) | 2.0 L, 2-1/8 US qt | [S1][S3] |
| Drain plug | 35 N·m (26 ft-lb), new gasket | [S2][S4] |
| Filler plug | 35 N·m (26 ft-lb), new gasket | [S2][S4] |

**The transfer case does not take the transmission's fluid.** The automatic transmission takes Matic J or Matic S; the transfer case takes Matic D [S1][S3]. One reply in the forum thread below says it is "the same as the auto trans", but both Nissan manuals contradict that [S1][S3][S5]. Nissan warns that any other fluid can hurt shift quality and damage the transfer case [S1].

## Tools

- A 10 mm hex (Allen) key or bit; it fits both plugs on at least one owner's truck [S5]
- Torque wrench
- Drain pan
- Fluid pump or squeeze bottle with a hose (the fill hole is on the side of the case) [S5]
- Two new plug gaskets [S1]

## Procedure

1. Park level and stop the engine [S1]. Leave the front end down; there is no need to jack it up [S5].
2. Find the transfer case behind the transmission, where the rear driveshaft starts. The fill plug is on the side of the case and the drain plug is on the bottom [S5].
3. Loosen the **filler plug first**, so you know you can refill before you drain [S5].
4. Remove the drain plug and gasket and let the fluid drain [S1][S2].
5. Fit the drain plug with a new gasket and torque it to 35 N·m (26 ft-lb) [S2].
6. Fill slowly through the filler hole until fluid reaches the bottom edge of the hole. Nissan says to take about 3 minutes over the fill [S2].
7. Wait 3 minutes, then check the level again and top it up [S2].
8. Fit the filler plug with a new gasket and torque it to 35 N·m (26 ft-lb) [S2].

## Notes from owners

- **They are often low.** One owner drained about 1.3 qt and put back about 2.1 qt. Another also found theirs "way low". Both said shifting into 4WD got quieter afterwards [S5].
- Some owners put a little silicone or RTV on the plug threads [S5]. Nissan specifies a new gasket, not sealant [S2].
- If the 4LO light keeps flashing after a shift, the transfer case did not finish shifting between 4H and 4LO and may be sitting in neutral. Nissan warns that the automatic's Park may not hold in that state, so do not leave the truck parked like that [S2].
