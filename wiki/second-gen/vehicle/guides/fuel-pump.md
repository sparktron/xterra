# Fuel pump, fuel filter and fuel level sender

[Guides](index.md) · [Reference home](../index.md) · Related: [Air, cabin and fuel filters](air-fuel-filters.md), [EVAP system faults](evap-system.md)

| | |
|---|---|
| Applies to | 2005-2015 Xterra. The fuel pump, fuel filter and fuel level sensor are one in-tank assembly, reached by lowering the tank [S1] |
| Common reason | A failing fuel level sender: the gauge reads erratically or slowly, and code P0463 sets [S3] |
| Difficulty | Moderate. 2.5-3 hours the first time, best with a helper [S2] |

## Specifications and parts

| Item | Value | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Fuel tank strap bolts (3) | 43.6 N·m (32 ft-lb) | [S1][S2] |
| Fuel tank shield (skid) bolts | 14.2 N·m (10 ft-lb) | [S1][S2] |
| Filler pipe bracket bolts | 4.5 N·m (40 in-lb) | [S1] |
| Fuel level sender alone, one owner's 2005 | Nissan 25060-ZS00A | [S2] |
| Pump-assembly O-ring ("packing"), one owner's 2005 | Nissan 17342-EA000; Nissan says always replace it | [S1][S2] |

**Check the recall first on a 2006 or 2008:** under NHTSA campaign **10V075000**, the tank shell could deform so the gauge read about a quarter tank when empty. Dealers replaced the sending unit with one with a modified float arm [S4]. See [Recalls](../recalls.md).

Nissan's manual replaces the whole pump assembly, and in 2011 one owner's dealer would only do that [S1][S3]. The fuel level **sender slides off the side of the pump** and is sold separately, so owners replace just that part [S2][S3].

## Before you start

- **Run the tank nearly empty.** It's lighter, and fuel spills out of the pump opening if the level is above it. Nissan drains about 15 L (4 US gal) from a full tank to get below the opening [S1][S2].
- **Relieve the fuel pressure:** pull the fuel pump fuse with the engine running and let it stall. One owner's was the 15 A fuse in the middle of the top row of the under-hood box; check the diagram on your cover [S1][S2].
- Remove the filler cap, then disconnect the battery [S1][S3].
- Hose off the underside first, so dirt doesn't fall into the open tank [S3].

## Procedure

1. Remove the left rear wheel, and the spare for room [S1][S3].
2. Remove the fuel tank shield (four 12 mm bolts on one owner's truck). The rear mounts have to be worked past the leaf spring and driveshaft [S1][S2].
3. Nissan's manual removes the rear driveshaft on the 2WD shaft (2S1330) [S1].
4. From the wheel well, loosen the clamp on the filler hose (10 mm) and twist the hose off. Unplug the two quick-connect lines; they are different types [S2]. Nissan: **push the tabs fully in and pull. Don't use tools on them or twist them more than needed, and bag the open ends** [S1].
5. Support the tank with a jack or stands. Remove the three strap bolts (two front, one rear with a hook or swivel) and lower the tank a few inches [S1][S2][S3].
   - Don't let the tank hang on its lines and wiring [S1][S3].
   - One owner found a front strap nut stripped from the factory [S3].
6. Reach over the tank to unplug the pump's connector (pry its latch up with a small screwdriver) and the feed line, then lower the tank the rest of the way [S1][S2].
7. Clean the top of the tank around the lock ring [S2].
8. **Mark the lock ring's position** relative to the tank. Remove it with Nissan's tool (J-45722), or tap it around with a screwdriver and hammer one notch at a time [S1][S2][S3].
9. Lift the assembly out **without bending the float arm** [S1].
10. To replace only the sender: note the wire routing, unplug the brown connector and the black wire clip, push in the small tab on the sender, and slide it off. Fit the new one the same way [S2].
11. Fit a **new O-ring**, keeping fuel off it. Lower the assembly in flush on your marks, and refit the lock ring fully engaged and as tight as it came off. A helper pressing down on the ring helps [S1][S2].
12. Reconnect the lines until they **click**, and tug to confirm. Nissan says check that both retainer tabs are engaged [S1][S2].
13. Raise the tank. **Route the small fuel line down the side of the tank where it has room**; one owner read of a line pinched between tank and frame. Refit the straps at 43.6 N·m (32 ft-lb) [S2].
14. Refit the filler hose and lines, the shield at 14.2 N·m (10 ft-lb), the fuse and the battery [S1][S2].
15. **Leak check:** turn the key ON without starting, and look for leaks with the system pressurized. Then start the engine, rev it above idle, and check every connection again [S1].
