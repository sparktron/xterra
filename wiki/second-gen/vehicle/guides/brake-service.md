## Torque values

Nissan changed several caliper values between manual years. The 2008 and 2013 manuals agree [S1][S6], and so do the 2007 manual and an owner's transcription of the 2005 manual [S4][S5]. The 2006 manual wasn't checked. Use the column for your truck's year.

| Fastener | 2008 and 2013 manuals | 2007 manual | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Front caliper sliding pin bolts | 26.5 N·m (20 ft-lb) | 44 N·m (32 ft-lb) | [S1][S5][S6] |
| Front torque member (caliper bracket) bolts | 185 N·m (136 ft-lb) | 185 N·m (136 ft-lb) | [S1][S5][S6] |
| Front brake hose union bolt (new copper washers) | 18.2 N·m (13 ft-lb) | 32 N·m (24 ft-lb) | [S1][S5][S6] |
| Rear caliper sliding pin bolts | 26.5 N·m (20 ft-lb) | 26 N·m (19 ft-lb) | [S1][S5][S6] |
| Rear torque member bolts | 88.3 N·m (65 ft-lb) | 102.5 N·m (76 ft-lb) | [S1][S5][S6] |
| Rear brake hose connection (new copper washer) | 18.2 N·m (13 ft-lb) | 26 N·m (19 ft-lb) | [S1][S5][S6] |
| Bleed valves | 7.85 N·m (69 in-lb) | 7.8 N·m (69 in-lb) | [S1][S5][S6] |

The parts may have changed along with the specification. If your calipers aren't original, check which caliper you have [Unverified].

## Front pads

1. Remove the wheel. Remove the reservoir cap and **partly drain the master cylinder reservoir**. Pushing the pistons back returns fluid to it [S1].
2. Remove the **lower** sliding pin bolt and swing the caliper body up. Hang it with wire so the hose doesn't take the weight. There is no need to remove the torque member or the hose for a pad change [S1].
   - Don't press the brake pedal with the pads out, or the pistons will pop out [S1].
3. Remove the inner and outer pads, shims, shim cover, pad return spring and pad retainers [S1].
4. Fit new shims and shim covers with the pads. Nissan says always to replace them as a set. Put Molykote AS-880N grease between the outer shim and the shim cover [S1].
5. Grease the upper and lower pad retainers with Molykote 7439 and press them on **flush with the torque member** [S1].
6. Fit the pads, shims and shim cover. Push the pistons back into the caliper while you watch the reservoir level [S1].
7. Hook the pad return spring into the holes at the bottom edge of the pads [S1].
8. Swing the caliper down and torque the sliding pin bolts [S1].
9. Check that the brake doesn't drag, top up the reservoir, and refit the wheel [S1].
10. **Pump the pedal until it is firm before you drive**, then bed the pads in (below) [S1].

## Rear pads

The rear is the same job with a single-piston caliper and no return spring: lower sliding pin out, caliper up, pads, shims and retainers out [S1]. Grease the inner pad's back plate and shim with AS-880N and the retainers with 7439, push the piston back, swing the caliper down and torque the pins [S1].

## Rotors

1. Remove the caliper body. To avoid opening the hydraulics, hang it by wire rather than disconnecting the hose. The manual disconnects the hose only when the caliper itself is coming off [S1].
2. Remove the torque member bolts and the torque member [S1].
3. If you will reuse the rotor, **mark its position on the hub** so it goes back the same way [S1].
4. Pull the rotor. Measure its thickness with a micrometer and replace it if it is under the minimum [S1].
5. Install the rotor, then the torque member, torqued to spec [S1].
6. Fit the pads and caliper as above. If you disconnected the hose, use new copper washers, torque the union bolt, and bleed the brakes [S1].
7. **Runout check:** with the rotor held on by at least two lug nuts, measure runout 10 mm (0.39 in) in from the edge. If it is over the limit, move the rotor one stud position on the hub and measure again. Refinish the rotor only if moving it doesn't fix it [S1].

## Bedding in new pads or rotors (Nissan's burnishing procedure)

Do this after new pads or rotors, after refinishing rotors, or if the pedal goes soft on new brakes [S1]. Only do it where it is safe to.

1. Drive straight at 50 km/h (31 mph) [S1].
2. Brake to a full stop with medium pedal effort, taking 3-5 seconds to stop [S1].
3. Drive at 50 km/h (31 mph) for 1 minute without stopping, to cool the brakes [S1].
4. Repeat 10 times or more [S1].

## Tips from owners

- The caliper slide pin bolts are about 13 mm and the torque member bolts 19 mm, per one owner [S4].
- If the pedal stays soft after a pad job, bleed the brakes; see [Brake fluid flush and bleeding](brake-fluid-bleed.md) [Unverified].
- Clean brake dust with a vacuum, not compressed air [S1].
