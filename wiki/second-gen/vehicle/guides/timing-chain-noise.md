# Timing chain whine (secondary chain tensioner shoe wear)

[Guides](index.md) · [Reference home](../index.md) · Fix: [Timing chain and guide replacement](timing-chain-replacement.md) · Related: [Serpentine belt and pulleys](serpentine-belt.md)

The VQ40DE has a primary timing chain from the crankshaft and a short **secondary** chain on each cylinder head that links its intake and exhaust camshafts. Each secondary chain has a hydraulic tensioner with a plastic "shoe". On early engines the chains wear through those shoes, and the engine develops a high-pitched whine or buzz from the front [S1][S2][S3].

| | |
|---|---|
| Applies to | Nissan's bulletin NTB09-128 applies to all 2005-2010 Xterra, and to 2005-2010 Frontier and Pathfinder with the VQ40 [S1] |
| The fix | Replace both secondary chains and both tensioner shoes, then change the oil and filter [S1] |
| Earlier bulletin | Nissan announced a first bulletin for the same noise, TSB 07042 "Buzzing/whining noise from timing chain area", on 2007-07-17 (NHTSA ID 10022421). NTB09-128 followed in 2009 [S1][S4] |
| Nissan's labor time | 4.4 hours on an Xterra [S1] |

## Symptoms

- A **high-frequency buzzing or whining** from the front of the engine, from the secondary timing chain system [S1].
- **It rises in pitch with engine speed.** Nissan says that if the noise does *not* change with rpm, the timing chains may not be the cause [S1].
- Owners describe it as a whine from the front of the motor. One heard it at almost 100,000 mi. Another's '07 got steadily louder by 50,000 mi [S2].
- Left long enough, the chain wears through the plastic shoe face and runs on metal [S2].

## Rule these out first

A whine from the front of the engine can also be the drive belt system: [check the tensioner and idler and spin each pulley](serpentine-belt.md), and listen with the belt off. One owner ruled out his alternator and belt tensioner before he found the chains [S2].

## What it costs and who does it

- One owner in 2011 was quoted **$1,600** by a dealer [S2].
- Another owner's dealer did it under the 6-year/60,000-mile powertrain warranty in 2012, on an '07 at 50,000 mi. The dealer said it did about two a week [S2].
- Doing it yourself takes a weekend the first time; see [Timing chain and guide replacement](timing-chain-replacement.md) [S2].
