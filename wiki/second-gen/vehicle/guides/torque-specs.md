# Torque specifications (summary)

[Guides](index.md) · [Reference home](../index.md)

The torque values used in these guides, in one place. Every value is from a Nissan service manual. The linked guide gives the page, the steps, and conditions such as "new nut" or "threads oiled".

**Nissan changed some values between model years.** Where the manuals differ, both are shown. If your year isn't covered, the nearest manual year is the best guide, but check yours. Values are N·m, with ft-lb or in-lb in brackets.
