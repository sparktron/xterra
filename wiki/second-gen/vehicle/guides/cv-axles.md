## Which driveshafts you have (2012 manual)

| Shaft | Model | Fitted to | Runout limit | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Front | 2F1310, 2 joints, flanges at both ends | all 4WD | 0.6 mm (0.024 in) | [S1] |
| Rear | 2S1330, aluminum tube, slip yoke into the transmission | 2WD automatic (S, X) | 1.02 mm (0.0402 in) | [S1] |
| Rear | 2S1330-BJ100, steel tube, slip yoke into the transfer case | 4WD | 0.60 mm (0.0236 in) | [S1] |

U-joint (journal) axial play must be 0.02 mm (0.0008 in) or less, and joint flex effort 2.26 N·m (20 in-lb) or less [S1].

## Driveshaft U-joints

**Symptoms:** clicking from the driveshaft, vibration at 40-70 mph, or trouble getting out of 4WD. One owner's failed front U-joint caused all three [S3]. A shaft vibration only at high speed should be checked for runout first [S1].

1. **Mark both flanges with paint** at each end so the shaft goes back in the same position [S1][S3].
2. For the rear shaft, put the transmission in neutral and release the parking brake [S1].
3. Remove the 4 nuts and bolts at each flange; one owner needed two 9/16 in wrenches because sockets didn't fit. Lower the shaft and mark which end is the front [S1][S3].
4. Mark the yokes, remove the snap rings, and press or tap out the bearing cups [S1][S3]. A bench U-joint press makes it easy [S3].
5. Grease the new bearing cups inside. Fit the new joint without letting the needles drop, and press it home [S1][S3].
6. Choose **new snap rings** that give the specified axial play. Nissan sizes them so the two sides differ by no more than 0.02 mm (0.0008 in). Tap the yoke to take the clearance to zero, then check that the joint moves smoothly [S1].
7. Refit the shaft on your marks with **new bolts and nuts**, torqued to the table. One owner needed a crowfoot to reach them [S1][S3].
8. Test drive for vibration. If it vibrates, Nissan's fix is to rotate the flange connection 90°, 180° or 270° and recheck the runout [S1].

**Parts:** in 2009 one owner found no Xterra listing at the parts stores and fitted joints listed for an '05 Pathfinder from NAPA [S3]. Owners recommend quality joints (Spicer, Precision) over cheap ones; a 2020 thread points to a university lecture series on why [S3][S6].

## Front CV axles (4WD)

**Replacing a CV axle** uses most of the [front wheel bearing](wheel-bearing-service.md#front-hub-and-bearing-2wd-and-4wd) procedure. Nissan's sequence [S2]:

1. Remove the wheel, the engine under cover, and the ABS sensor harness from its mount. Don't pull on the harness [S2].
2. Remove the hub and bearing assembly. The ABS sensor can stay in the hub if the hub isn't being replaced [S2].
3. Separate the upper ball joint from the knuckle with a separator tool and support the lower arm on a jack [S2].
4. Pry the inner end out of the front diff. Remove the diff side oil seal [S2].
5. Check the joints for roughness or looseness and the boots for cracks or leaking grease [S2].
6. Install a **new diff side seal**, and protect it with a seal guide as the axle goes in. Tap the axle home until it locks [S2].

**Replacing a CV boot** [S2][S4]:

1. With the axle on a bench in a vise (soft jaws), remove the boot bands and slide the boot back [S2].
2. **Inner (diff) end:** mark the housing to the shaft, remove the stopper ring and pull the housing off. Then remove the snap ring and slide off the ball cage, balls and inner race as a set; don't lose the balls [S2][S4].
3. **Outer (wheel) end:** thread a slide hammer at least 30 mm (1.18 in) into the joint and pull it straight off. Nissan says to replace the whole axle if it won't come off after five tries [S2]. One owner used a length of 1-1/4 in pipe over the shaft as a hammer instead [S4].
4. Clean out the old grease. Replace the whole joint if it has any rough rotation, cracks or unusual looseness [S2].
5. Tape the splines, slide on the **new** boot and bands, and remove the tape [S2].
6. Pack the joint with the specified grease [S2]:

| Item | Inner (final drive side) | Outer (wheel side) | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Joint type | Rzeppa | Rzeppa | [S2] |
| Grease quantity | 120-140 g (4.23-4.94 oz) | 110-130 g (3.88-4.59 oz) | [S2] |
| Boot installed length (L) | 163.9-164.3 mm (6.45-6.47 in) | 135.1 mm (5.32 in) | [S2] |
| Boot band crimp dimension (M) | 1.0-4.0 mm (0.039-0.157 in) | 1.0-4.0 mm (0.039-0.157 in) | [S2] |

7. Keep grease **off the boot's sealing grooves**, or the boot can slip off [S2].
8. Set the boot to its installed length, burping out trapped air with a blunt tool, then crimp new bands [S2].
9. Fit **new** snap rings, stopper ring, circlips and dust cover; Nissan says none are reusable [S2].

**Lifted trucks:** more than about 2 in of front lift runs the stock CV axles at steep angles. Owners going higher fit Titan-length axles [S7].
