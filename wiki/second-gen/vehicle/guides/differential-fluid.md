# Front and rear differential fluid change

| | |
|---|---|
| Applies to | 2005-2015 Xterra: front diff on 4WD trucks only, rear diff on every truck [S1][S4] |
| Difficulty | Easy. The hard parts are getting the plugs out and pumping fluid in uphill [S3] |
| Time | About an hour for both diffs [Unverified] |
| Interval | On Nissan's severe-use Schedule 1, inspect every 15,000 mi (24,000 km). If you tow, carry a roof load, or drive rough or muddy roads, change it every 30,000 mi (48,000 km) or 24 months [S1] |

## Which differentials you have

- **Front (4WD only): R180A.** Every 4WD Xterra uses it [S1][S4].
- **Rear: C200 or M226.** The M226 is the heavier axle, and owners usually call it the Dana 44 [S3]. The electronic rear locker (E-Lock) is built into an M226 [S2].
- The 2014-2015 owner's manuals list the M226 fill (75W-140, 4-1/4 pt) for manual-transmission trucks and for automatics with the E-Lock, and the C200 fill (75W-90, 3-3/8 pt) for automatics without it [S5][S6]. The 2010-2013 manuals split the same two fills by transmission only. See [Fluids and capacities](../fluids-capacities.md).
- Owners put it simply: every Off-Road, PRO-4X and manual-transmission truck has the M226 from the factory [S7].
- If you are not sure, look at the plugs. On the C200 the drain plug takes a 10 mm hex (Allen) key and the fill plug a 14 mm hex [S3].

## Specifications

| Item | Front R180A | Rear C200 | Rear M226 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Gear oil | API GL-5 80W-90, or SAE 90 in hot climates above 32°F (0°C) [S1] | API GL-5 synthetic 75W-90 [S1] | API GL-5 synthetic 75W-140 [S1] |
| Capacity (approximate) | 0.85 L, 1-3/4 US pt [S1] | 1.6 L, 3-3/8 US pt [S1] | 2.01 L, 4-1/4 US pt [S1] |
| Drain plug | 34.5 N·m (25 ft-lb), new gasket [S2][S4] | 49 N·m (36 ft-lb), new gasket [S1][S2][S4] | 43 N·m (32 ft-lb), thread sealant [S1][S2] |
| Filler plug | 34.5 N·m (25 ft-lb), new gasket [S2][S4] | 49 N·m (36 ft-lb), thread sealant [S1][S2][S4] | 43 N·m (32 ft-lb), thread sealant [S1][S2] |

The M226 row is from the 2012 manual, both with and without the locker, and from the 2006 manual's E-Lock axle [S2][S4]. The 2006 manual's M226 *without* the locker gives different numbers: drain plug 27 N·m (20 ft-lb), filler plug 34 N·m (25 ft-lb) [S4]. If you have an early truck without the locker, use those.

The front diff takes 1-3/4 pt, so a full service of both diffs needs about 3 quarts in total [S1][S3]. Buy new plug gaskets before you start. One owner found Nissan did not stock them and matched them at NAPA [S3].

## Tools

- A ratchet whose square drive fits the front plugs, and hex (Allen) bits for the C200: 10 mm for the drain and 14 mm for the fill [S3]. A 14 mm hex bit is hard to find in stores; one owner ordered it as McMaster-Carr 5571A71 [S3].
- Torque wrench
- Drain pan or bags. One owner drains each diff into a clear zip bag so he can see the fluid and the metal in it [S3].
- Hand-pump gear oil bottle or squeeze pump. The fill holes are on the side of the housing and you are pumping uphill [S3].
- Thread sealant for the rear plugs (Nissan specifies its High Performance Thread Sealant or equivalent) [S1]
- Jack stands if you lift the truck. Keep it level for the final fill check [S1].

## Procedure

Do both diffs the same way: fill plug out first, then drain, then refill.

1. Park on level ground with the engine off. The manual says never to check the level with the engine running [S1].
2. Front diff: remove the front skid plate or under cover to reach the R180A plugs [S3].
3. **Loosen the filler plug before you drain.** If a seized fill plug won't come out, you can still drive on a full diff. You can't drive on an empty one [Unverified].
4. Remove the drain plug and gasket and let the oil drain. Wipe the magnet on the plug and look at what was on it [S1][S3].
5. Fit the drain plug. Use a new gasket on the R180A and C200. On the M226, put thread sealant on the threads. Torque it to the table value [S1][S2].
6. Pump new gear oil into the filler hole until it reaches the bottom edge of the hole, which is the level the manual specifies [S1][S3].
7. Fit the filler plug: a new gasket on the R180A, thread sealant on the C200 and M226. Torque it to the table value [S1][S2].
8. Refit the skid plate. After a short drive, check both plugs for leaks [Unverified].

## What to expect and what to watch for

- **Metal paste on the first change is normal.** A fine grey paste on the drain plug at the first change is gears breaking in. It should be much less at the next change [S3].
- **Check that the front diff is full.** One owner found his front diff about a pint low at 30,000 mi without a visible leak [S3].
- **Lifting the rear helps.** One owner raises the rear to fill the rear diff so it takes its full capacity [S3].
- **After re-gearing**, change the oil again after the break-in miles [S3].
- **Water crossings:** if water may have got into a diff, change the oil. The front diff's breather is a known weak spot; see [Lockers and traction](lockers-traction.md) and the forum write-ups on breather extensions [Unverified].
