# Engine and underhood noise diagnosis

[Guides](index.md) · [Reference home](../index.md) · Related: [Timing chain whine](timing-chain-noise.md), [Serpentine belt and pulleys](serpentine-belt.md), [Driveline and wheel bearing noises](driveline-noises.md)

## Which noise is it?

| Noise | Most likely | Check | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| High-pitched **whine or buzz from the front of the engine, rising with rpm** | Secondary timing chain tensioner shoe wear (2005-2010 especially) | See [Timing chain whine](timing-chain-noise.md) | [S1] |
| Buzzy **rattle from the top of the engine, under acceleration around 1,500-3,000 rpm** | The VIAS power valve actuator on the intake plenum | Below | [S2][S3] |
| Rattle at idle when the **A/C compressor clutch** clicks on or off | A failing A/C compressor | Switch the A/C on and off and listen | [S4] |
| Clatter when the fan clutch engages, especially at idle | Fan or lower fan shroud loose, or the fan hitting the shroud | Check the lower shroud's clips | [S4] |
| Squeal or chirp at start-up | Drive belt or tensioner | See [Serpentine belt](serpentine-belt.md) | [S5] |
| Rattle from **underneath**, at certain rpm | Loose heat shields on the exhaust and catalysts | Tap the shields and clamp any loose ones | [S3] |
| Clunk or rattle over bumps and in turns, from the engine area | Engine mounts or a loose bolt; front suspension parts | Have someone rock the truck while you listen underneath | [S6] |
| Ticking that rises with rpm | An exhaust manifold leak, or valve clearance. Nissan schedules no valve adjustment | Nissan checks valve clearance only if valve noise increases | [S7] |

## VIAS rattle

The **VIAS (variable induction air system) power valve** in the intake plenum switches the intake runner length. Its actuator is on the outside of the plenum [S2][S8]. Over time it can rattle, a buzzy noise from the top of the engine under load [S2][S3].

- Nissan issued a bulletin: **TSB 05015A, "Rattle noise from VIAS power valve actuator / intake manifold collector"** (NHTSA ID 10012840) [S9]. Owners describe the bulletin fix as epoxying the valve housing inside the manifold, about 20 minutes plus 2 hours for the epoxy to cure. With that fix the valve keeps all its movement [S2].
- **An owner's low-cost fix:** the actuator has an external plastic fin that rests between two fixed fins on the plenum, and pulling it counterclockwise stops the rattle. He drilled the fin and threaded in a coarse screw (from a mud flap) so its tip holds the fin off the fixed fin. It stopped the rattle and left the valve about 95% of its travel [S2]. Other owners preferred the bulletin's fix [S2].
- Replacement actuator: Nissan **14510-EA21A**. One owner found a vacuum leak at the actuator with a smoke machine [S8].
- **Rodents:** one owner's VIAS solenoid wiring and its vacuum hoses to the vacuum tank were chewed through by a squirrel nesting on the engine. Replacing the vacuum hose by the foot and splicing the wires with heat-shrink connectors fixed it [S10].
- A VIAS solenoid circuit fault sets **P1800** [S11].
