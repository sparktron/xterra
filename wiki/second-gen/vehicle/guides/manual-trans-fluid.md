# Manual transmission fluid change (6-speed FS6R31A)

| | |
|---|---|
| Applies to | 2005-2015 Xterra with the 6-speed manual (FS6R31A) [S1][S2] |
| Difficulty | Easy [Unverified] |
| Time | 30-45 minutes [Unverified] |
| Interval | On Nissan's severe-use Schedule 1, inspect every 15,000 mi (24,000 km). If you tow, carry a roof load, or drive rough or muddy roads, change it every 30,000 mi (48,000 km) or 24 months [S1] |

## Specifications

| Item | Value | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Fluid | Genuine Nissan Manual Transmission Fluid (MTF) HQ Multi 75W-85, or API GL-4 75W-85. The 2006 manual also allows 75W-90 | [S1][S3] |
| Capacity, 4WD | 4.18 L, 8-7/8 US pt | [S1][S3] |
| Capacity, 2WD (2006 manual) | 3.98 L, 8-3/8 US pt | [S3] |
| Drain plug | 34.5 N·m (25 ft-lb), new gasket | [S2] |
| Filler plug | 34.5 N·m (25 ft-lb), new gasket | [S2] |

GL-4 is specified, not GL-5. GL-5 additives can be hard on synchronizer brass [Unverified].

## Procedure

1. Drive or idle the truck to warm the transmission, then stop the engine [S2].
2. Loosen the **filler plug first**, so you know you can refill [Unverified].
3. Remove the drain plug and drain the oil. The case has a magnet inside; look at what the old oil carried out [S2].
4. Fit the drain plug with a **new gasket** and torque it to 34.5 N·m (25 ft-lb) [S2].
5. Remove the filler plug and pump in new oil until it reaches the bottom of the filler hole [S2].
6. **Do not start the engine** while you check the level [S1].
7. Fit the filler plug with a new gasket and torque it to 34.5 N·m (25 ft-lb) [S2].
8. Check for leaks after a short drive [S1].
