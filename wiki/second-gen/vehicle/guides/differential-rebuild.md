# Differential rebuild, re-gear and locker installation

| | |
|---|---|
| Applies to | R180A front, C200 rear and M226 rear diffs on 2005-2015 Xterra [S1] |
| Difficulty | Ring-and-pinion setup is precision work: pinion depth, bearing preload and backlash all have to be set and measured. Many owners send it to a gear shop. A drop-in or spider-gear locker in an open carrier is within reach of a careful home mechanic [S1][S2] |

On **both** M226 versions, Nissan's manual lists much of the axle as **non-serviceable**: the drive pinion assembly, side bearing caps, adjuster lock plates and differential case on both, the gear carrier on the open axle, and the lock solenoid on the E-Lock axle [S1]. Gear shops do set these axles up, but the factory manual doesn't cover it [Unverified].

The pinion nut is a **range** because it sets the collapsible spacer: tighten it in steps until the pinion bearing preload is in spec, and never back it off. If you overshoot, the spacer has to be replaced [Unverified].

## Example: installing a Lokka locker in an R180A front diff

An owner's procedure on a bench-mounted R180A, about 1-2 hours [S2]:

**Tools:** 17 mm and 14 mm sockets, feeler gauges, snap-ring pliers, a pry bar, small screwdrivers, a punch, a torque wrench, a razor blade and 400-grit paper [S2].

1. Drain the diff. Pry out the passenger axle seal (replace it if damaged), remove the outer snap ring and slide out the half shaft [S2].
2. Remove the 14 mm cover bolts and pry the cover off at its pry slot [S2].
3. **Mark the carrier bearing caps** before you remove their four 17 mm bolts; Nissan marks the driver's side with green paint [S2].
4. Lift the carrier out. **Note where the shims and thrust washers go**: two on the ring-gear (passenger) side, one on the driver's side. They must go back the same way [S2].
5. Remove the ring gear's 17 mm bolts; tap it side to side to free it. This is also where you can read the ratio stamped on the ring gear [S2].
6. Drive out the roll pin through the access hole, slide out the cross pin, and rotate the spider gears out. Keep the flat thrust washers; the dished ones aren't reused [S2].
7. Fit the locker per its instructions, refit the ring gear and carrier with their original shims, and torque everything to the table above. Clean both gasket faces and re-seal the cover [S1][S2].
