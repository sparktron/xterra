# Air filter, cabin filter and fuel filter

[Guides](index.md) · [Reference home](../index.md) · [Part numbers](../parts.md)

| | |
|---|---|
| Applies to | 2005-2015 Xterra [S1][S4] |
| Difficulty | Easy. About 5 minutes for each filter [S4] |
| Interval, engine air filter | Replace every 30,000 mi (48,000 km), sooner in dusty conditions [S1] |
| Interval, cabin filters | Replace every 15,000 mi (24,000 km) or 12 months [S1] |
| Fuel filter | Not a maintenance item. It is part of the fuel pump and level sensor assembly inside the tank [S1] |

## Engine air filter

1. Remove the engine room cover, if your truck has one [S1].
2. Disconnect the air duct and resonator from the upper half of the air cleaner case [S1].
3. Unplug the MAF/IAT sensor connector [S1].
4. Unhook the clips and lift the upper half of the case [S1].
5. Lift out the filter and fit the new one, seated all the way round [S1].
6. Reassemble in reverse order and refit the MAF connector [S1].

If you run an oiled aftermarket filter, don't over-oil it. Oil carried onto the MAF sensor can upset its readings [Unverified].

## Cabin (in-cabin micro) filters

There are **two** filter pieces, sold in one pack. The filter is split so it fits through the small opening [S1][S4].

1. Open the **lower** glove box. Press in its sides until the stops clear and let it hang down on its cord [S1][S4].
   - It takes more force than you'd expect [S4].
2. Behind it, on the housing above the blower motor, is the filter cover. Gently lift its tab and pull the cover down and off [S1][S4].
   - **The tab is brittle.** Owners who break it hold the cover on with a zip tie [S5].
   - Some trucks also have a screw on the cover; remove it if present [S4].
3. Pull out both filters. One sits off to the right [S1][S4].
4. Slide the first new filter in and push it over to the right, then fit the second [S1].
5. **Arrows down.** The filters are marked with airflow arrows. Nissan says the arrow end faces the rear of the truck with the arrows pointing down [S1].
6. Refit the cover until the tab locks, then close the glove box [S1].

One owner found his filters very dirty at the 15,000 mi change after off-road use, and now checks them every 10,000 mi [S4].

## Fuel filter

There is no separate fuel filter to change. It is built into the fuel pump, fuel level sensor and filter assembly in the tank, and is replaced only with that assembly. Release the fuel pressure before opening the system [S1][S2]. See [Fuel pump and sending unit](fuel-pump.md).
