| Level check | the reservoir's HOT band for fluid at 50-80°C (122-176°F), the COLD band for 0-30°C (32-86°F) | [S1] |

Nissan's manual cautions against reusing drained power steering fluid and against overfilling [S1].

## Checking the level

1. Read the level against the scale on the reservoir. Use the HOT band when the fluid is warm and the COLD band when it is cold [S1].
2. Top up with the specified fluid; don't overfill [S1].

## Checking for leaks (Nissan's procedure)

1. Idle the engine until the reservoir fluid reaches 50-80°C (122-176°F) [S1].
2. Turn the wheel lock to lock several times, holding it at each lock for 5 seconds, and look for leaks [S1].
   - **Never hold the wheel at full lock for more than 10 seconds.** It can damage the pump [S1].
   - Keep the reservoir above MIN throughout [S1].
3. If a fitting leaks, loosen and retighten it, but don't over-tighten: that damages the O-ring or washer. Replace copper washers rather than reusing them [S1].
4. Fluid collected in the steering gear boots means the gear itself is leaking [S1].

## Changing the fluid (reservoir exchange)

Nissan gives no flush procedure. Owners exchange it in stages [Unverified]:

1. Pull the reservoir up off its bracket. It isn't bolted, just a tight fit [S4].
2. Remove the cap and suck out as much old fluid as you can [Unverified].
3. For a more complete change, disconnect the **return** hose at the reservoir. Plug the reservoir's nipple, run the hose into a container, and keep the reservoir topped up while a helper idles the engine for a few seconds at a time. Stop when clean fluid comes out [Unverified].
   - The metal nipples are thin and kink easily, so ease the hoses off rather than yanking them [S4].
4. Reconnect everything, top up, and bleed the air out (below) [S1].

## Bleeding the air out (Nissan's procedure)

Signs of air: bubbles in the reservoir, a clicking noise or excessive buzzing from the pump [S1].

1. Check for leaks first [S1].
2. Start the engine and turn the wheel fully left and right several times, keeping the reservoir above MIN [S1].
3. At idle, hold the wheel at each lock for **3 seconds**. Repeat several times at about 3-second intervals [S1].
4. Look for bubbles, cloudy fluid or leaks. Repeat step 3 until the fluid is clear [S1].
5. Stop the engine and check the level [S1].

Some noise from the pump or gear while parked or turning slowly is normal, says Nissan [S1].
