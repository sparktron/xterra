## Specifications

| Item | Value | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Capacity, drain and refill with filter | 5.1 L, 5-3/8 US qt | [S1][S3] |
| Capacity without filter | 4.8 L, 5-1/8 US qt | [S1][S3] |
| Oil | SAE 5W-30 with the API Certification Mark; the 2013 manual recommends Genuine Nissan Ester Oil | [S1] |
| Drain plug | 34.3 N·m (25 ft-lb), new washer every time | [S2] |
| Oil filter | Hand-tight until the gasket touches, then 2/3 turn more, or 17.7 N·m (13 ft-lb) | [S1] |

Nissan's filter has a built-in pressure relief valve. Use a Genuine Nissan filter or an equivalent [S1]. Filter and drain-plug washer part numbers are on the [reference page](../oil-change.md#parts-nissan-part-numbers).

## Tools

- 14 mm wrench or socket for the drain plug [S4]
- Oil filter wrench. Nissan's tool is KV10115801 (J-38956), a cap-type wrench [S1]
- Drain pan, rags, and a small trash bag for the filter (tip below) [S4]
- Torque wrench
- About 5-1/2 qt of oil [S1][S4]

## Procedure

1. Warm the engine, check for leaks, then shut it off and wait **at least 10 minutes** [S1].
2. Remove the oil filler cap [S1].
3. Open the access door in the engine under cover. On aftermarket skid plates, use their access hole; Shrockworks and BP skids have one [S1][S4].
4. Remove the drain plug and let the oil drain [S1].
5. Clean the drain plug, fit a **new copper washer**, and torque it to 34.3 N·m (25 ft-lb) [S1][S2].
6. Loosen the filter with the filter wrench. One owner reaches it from the passenger side, lying with his right hip next to the right front tire [S4]. Keep oil off the drive belt and wipe up any that lands on the engine [S1].
   - **Tip:** once the filter is loose, slip a small kitchen trash bag over your hand and spin the filter off inside the bag. It catches almost all the mess [S4].
7. Wipe the filter's mounting surface clean. Oil the new filter's seal and screw it on by hand until it touches, then turn it 2/3 turn more [S1].
8. Refill with oil through the filler. The refill amount depends on oil temperature and how long it drained, so use 5-3/8 qt as a guide and finish with the dipstick [S1].
9. Start the engine, check that the oil pressure warning goes out, and check the plug and filter for leaks [S1][S4].
10. Stop the engine, wait **more than 10 minutes**, and check the dipstick. The level must be between L and H. Do not overfill [S1].
11. Refit the access door [S1].

## What to look for

- **Milky white oil** almost certainly means coolant is getting into the oil. Find the cause before you drive [S2].
- If you overfill, drain the excess. One owner simply removes and refits the filter to let some out [S4].
