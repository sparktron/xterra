# Axle ratios and re-gearing for bigger tires

## Factory axle ratios (2012 service manual)

| Axle | Transmission and grade | Ratio | Ring/pinion teeth | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Front R180A | Automatic, S / X / SV | 3.133 | 45/17 | [S1] |
| Front R180A | Automatic, PRO-4X | 3.357 | 47/14 | [S1] |
| Front R180A | Manual, S | 3.538 | 46/13 | [S1] |
| Front R180A | Manual, PRO-4X | 3.692 | 48/13 | [S1] |
| Rear C200 | Automatic 2WD | 2.937 | 47/16 | [S1] |
| Rear C200 | Automatic 4WD | 3.133 | 45/17 | [S1] |
| Rear M226 | Manual (no E-Lock) | 3.538 | 46/13 | [S1] |
| Rear M226 with E-Lock | Automatic 4WD | 3.357 | 47/14 | [S1] |
| Rear M226 with E-Lock | Manual 4WD | 3.692 | 48/13 | [S1] |

The 2015 press kit agrees for its trims: 3.692 with the manual, and 3.133 with the automatic (3.357 on PRO-4X) [S2]. Earlier model years may differ; check yours as below [Unverified].

**Front and rear ratios must match on a 4WD truck.** With mismatched axles, the front and rear tires fight each other in 4WD: wheel hop, a jerking steering wheel, and drivetrain stress [S3][S4]. This often trips people up when swapping an Off-Road or PRO-4X M226 into a truck that came with 3.133 gears. The front diff has to be changed to match [S4].

## Checking your ratio

- **Count the teeth.** The ring gear is usually stamped with both tooth counts; divide the ring gear teeth by the pinion teeth. For example, 47 and 15 stamped on a ring gear means 47 ÷ 15 = 3.13 [S5].
- **Count the driveshaft turns.** With the truck in 2WD and one wheel off the ground, turn the tire exactly **two** turns and count the driveshaft's turns. That count is the ratio, because an open diff turns the lifted wheel twice as fast. About 3-1/2 turns means 3.54 [S3]. With both wheels lifted, one turn of both wheels should give about 3-1/3 driveshaft turns on a 3.357 axle [S3].

## Bigger tires and the effective ratio

Larger tires make the effective gearing taller, by the ratio of the tire diameters. To get the original effective gearing back, multiply the stock axle ratio by (new tire diameter ÷ stock tire diameter) and pick the nearest available ratio [Unverified]. Remember that the speedometer also reads low on bigger tires [Unverified].

Common paths owners take:

- **Swap in factory gears.** Moving from 3.133 to the factory 3.357 or 3.692 means changing **both** axles to a matched set: an M226 and R180A from an Off-Road or PRO-4X, or from a manual truck [S1][S4].
- **Aftermarket ring-and-pinion** sets in either axle. This is precision work; see [Differential rebuild](differential-rebuild.md) for Nissan's setup specifications [S6].
- **Titan axles** for very large tires; see [Titan axle swaps](titan-axle-swap.md).
