# Ball joints and tie rod ends

[Guides](index.md) · [Reference home](../index.md) · Related: [Control arms and bushings](control-arms-bushings.md), [Wheel bearings](wheel-bearing-service.md)

| | |
|---|---|
| Applies to | 2005-2015 Xterra, front suspension and PR26AM rack-and-pinion steering [S1][S2] |
| Key fact | **The ball joints aren't sold separately by Nissan.** They are part of the upper and lower control arms ("links"), which are replaced as a unit [S1] |
| After the job | Get a wheel alignment. Nissan also resets the steering angle sensor's neutral position after an alignment [S1][S2] |

## Checking the ball joints (Nissan's method)

Replace the arm if the ball stud is worn, the joint is stiff to swing, or it has any axial play [S1]. Turn the lower ball joint at least **10 revolutions** to break it in before measuring [S1].

| Check | Upper ball joint | Lower ball joint | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Swinging force (spring scale at the cotter-pin hole, upper; at the groove, lower) | 8.1-79 N (1.82-17.8 lbf) | 11.4-145.5 N (2.56-32.71 lbf) | [S1] |
| Turning torque | 0.5-4.9 N·m (5-43 in-lb) | 0.5-6.4 N·m (5-56 in-lb) | [S1] |
| Vertical end play | 0 mm | 0 mm | [S1] |

A torn ball joint boot lets in grit and water. Replace the boot and its clamp if it's damaged [S1].

## Checking the tie rod ends (Nissan's method)

| Check | Outer tie rod end | Inner tie rod | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Swing torque | 0.3-2.9 N·m (3-25 in-lb), at the stud's cotter-pin hole | 1.0-7.8 N·m (9-69 in-lb), measured 83.2 mm (3.276 in) out | [S2] |
| Rotating torque | 0.3-2.9 N·m (3-25 in-lb) | | [S2] |
| Axial end play under a 490 N (110 lbf) load | 0.5 mm (0.020 in) or less | 0.2 mm (0.008 in) or less; the manual misprints the inch figure as 0.08 in | [S2] |

Replace a joint that is outside these values [S2]. In practice, any clunk or play you can feel by hand at the tie rod end with the wheel off the ground is too much [Unverified].

## Torque values

| Fastener | Torque | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Upper ball joint nut, new cotter pin | 78.5 N·m (58 ft-lb), 2006 and 2012 manuals | [S1][S3] |
| Lower ball joint pinch bolt nut, replaced | 128 N·m (94 ft-lb) in the 2012 manual; 95 N·m (70 ft-lb) in the 2006 manual | [S1][S3] |
| Outer tie rod end to knuckle nut, new cotter pin | 85.4 N·m (63 ft-lb) | [S2] |
| Outer tie rod jam (lock) nut | 88.5 N·m (65 ft-lb) | [S2] |
| Inner tie rod to rack, with thread locker | 88.5 N·m (65 ft-lb) | [S2] |
| Steering gear to crossmember bolts | 190 N·m (140 ft-lb) | [S2] |

One lift-kit write-up says to torque the upper ball joint nut to 100 ft-lb [S4]. That is well above Nissan's 78.5 N·m (58 ft-lb), so use the manual's value [S1].

## Replacing an outer tie rod end

1. Set the wheels straight ahead and lift the front [S2].
2. Count the turns, or measure from the jam nut to the stud, so the new end can go on at the same length. This keeps the toe close enough to drive to an alignment shop [Unverified].
3. Remove the cotter pin and loosen the nut at the knuckle. Separate the joint with a puller (Nissan tool HT72520000). **Leave the nut threaded on the end** so the threads aren't damaged, and don't tear the boot [S2].
4. Loosen the jam nut and unscrew the old end from the inner tie rod [S2].
5. Thread on the new end to the same length. Nissan's maximum inner tie rod length "L" is 84.0 mm (3.31 in) [S2].
6. Torque the jam nut to 88.5 N·m (65 ft-lb) and the knuckle nut to 85.4 N·m (63 ft-lb), and fit a **new** cotter pin [S2].
7. Get an alignment [S2].

## Replacing a ball joint (control arm)

See [Control arms and bushings](control-arms-bushings.md) for removing and refitting the upper and lower arms. Aftermarket upper arms with a replaceable or greaseable ball joint, and Titan arms, are popular on lifted trucks [S5].
