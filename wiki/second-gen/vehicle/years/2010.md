> Packages folded into trims: S and SE get the X Gear content, SE and Off-Road the Technology content. The S loses its manual option. [S6]

## What's new for 2010

- X Gear Package content (fog lights, roof rack crossbars, gear basket; plus first-aid kit and cargo net on SE) becomes standard on S and SE [S6]
- Technology Package content (Rockford Fosgate audio with 6-CD changer, eight speakers and subwoofer, Bluetooth, XM) becomes standard on SE and Off-Road [S6]
- The S is no longer offered with the manual transmission [S6]
- Exterior colors cut to six [S6]
- No factory options are offered [S6]

## Trims and equipment

- X: VDC, ABS, power windows and locks, remote keyless entry, cruise control, 60/40 split second row [S6]
- S: 16-inch alloys with BFGoodrich Long Trail tires, lumbar support, Sandblast Aluminum step rails, Easy Clean cargo surface, C-channel floor, front tow hook (4x4) [S6]
- Off-Road: roof-rack off-road lights, gray/red cloth, 265/75R16 BFGoodrich Rugged Trail T/A tires, Hill Descent Control and Hill Start Assist, electronic locking rear differential, Bilstein shocks, skid plates [S6]
- SE: 17-inch alloys with 265/65R17 BFGoodrich Long Trail tires, leather seats, trip computer, auto-dimming mirror with compass [S6]

|---|---|---|

| Automatic transmission fluid | Inspect every 15,000 mi / 12 mo (standard); severe schedule lists start 15,000 mi / 12 mo, inspect 15,000 mi / 12 mo, replace 15,000 mi / 12 mo | [S210] |
| Manual transmission oil | Inspect every 15,000 mi / 12 mo (standard); severe schedule lists start 15,000 mi / 12 mo, inspect 15,000 mi / 12 mo, replace 15,000 mi / 12 mo | [S210] |

| Differential oil | Inspect every 15,000 mi / 12 mo (standard); severe schedule lists start 15,000 mi / 12 mo, inspect 15,000 mi / 12 mo, replace 15,000 mi / 12 mo | [S210] |
| Brake fluid | Not listed for this year on Nissan's site | [S210] |
| Tires | Rotate every 7,500 mi | [S210] |
| Drive belts | Inspect from 60,000 mi / 48 mo, then every 15,000 mi / 12 mo | [S210] |

Severe service, per the owner's manual, covers repeated short trips in cold weather, dusty roads, extensive idling, towing and stop-and-go commuting [S115]. How Nissan's 'start / inspect / replace' sequences read is explained on [Maintenance schedule](../maintenance-schedule.md).
