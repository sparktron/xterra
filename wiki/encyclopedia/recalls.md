# Recalls

[Encyclopedia home](index.md)

Every campaign NHTSA's recall service lists for the 2005-2015 Xterra, as of this page's access date [S405][S406][S407][S408][S409][S410][S411][S412][S413][S414][S415]. Several are equipment campaigns for aftermarket or replacement parts, so they apply only to vehicles fitted with those parts. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls or a Nissan dealer to see open campaigns for your vehicle.

| Campaign | Model years | Component | What happened and the remedy | Filed | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 10E019000 | 2005-2010 | Replacement lower control links | Front and rear lower links sold as replacement parts between November 25, 2009 and May 14, 2010 could have weak collar welds; dealers replaced affected links. | 2010-05-20 | [S405][S406][S407][S408][S409][S410] |
| 10V517000 | 2005, 2006 | IPDM ECM relay | The ECM relay inside the intelligent power distribution module (IPDM) could develop silicon oxide on its contacts and cause stalling; dealers replaced the relay. | 2010-10-28 | [S405][S406] |
| 08V690000 | 2005-2009 | Front crash zone sensor (salt states) | In heavy road-salt states, salt water could enter the front crash zone sensor housing and corrode it, lighting the air bag warning lamp and risking non-deployment of the front air bags; dealers fitted a redesigned sensor, and owners elsewhere got an extended sensor warranty. | 2008-12-29 | [S405][S406][S407][S408][S409] |
| 05V458000 | 2005 | Nissan accessory tow hitch | Some welded joints in the dealer-accessory tow hitch were of poor quality and the hitch could separate while towing; dealers replaced the hitch. | 2005-10-05 | [S405] |
| 07E046000 | 2005, 2006 | Hopkins trailer brake harness (aftermarket) | Equipment recall: some Hopkins trailer-brake connection harnesses had a wire in the wrong connector position, sending brake-controller output to the dash-light circuit instead of the trailer brakes; Hopkins replaced the harness. | 2007-07-03 | [S405][S406] |
| 05E062000 | 2005 | Valley Industries hitch (aftermarket) | Equipment recall: Valley Class III receiver hitch P/N 82850 built March-September 2005 for the 2005 Xterra had weak welds and could break; Valley replaced it. | 2005-09-30 | [S405] |
| 10V075000 | 2006, 2008 | Fuel level sender | On some 2006 and 2008 vehicles the molded fuel tank shell could deform so the gauge read about a quarter tank when empty; dealers replaced the sending unit with one having a modified float arm. | 2010-03-03 | [S406][S408] |
| 08V045000 | 2008 | Tire placard | The tire placard listed an incorrect vehicle capacity weight (FMVSS 110); Nissan mailed a corrected placard. | 2008-01-25 | [S408] |
| 10V401000 | 2008-2010 | Garmin nuvi 750 navigation unit | Vehicles equipped with a Garmin nuvi 750 unit: the GPS battery could overheat; Garmin replaced the battery and added a spacer. | 2010-09-10 | [S408][S409][S410] |
| 07V528000 | 2008 | ABS/VDC actuator | Some ABS/VDC actuators were built with more than one ball in the valve section, which could block fluid flow and reduce front brake force or affect ABS/VDC; dealers replaced the actuator. | 2007-11-16 | [S408] |
| 07E105000 | 2008 | Continental ABS/ESC module (supplier) | Supplier equipment recall: some Continental ABS/ESC modules had extra check balls in hydraulic valves that could restrict brake fluid flow. | 2007-12-13 | [S408] |
| 23V067000 | 2008-2011 | Driver air bag emblem | The resin emblem on the driver's air bag cover could crack and detach when the air bag deploys; dealers replace the cover (Nissan campaigns PC944, PC945, PC946). | 2023-02-10 | [S408][S409][S410][S411] |
| 10E043000 | 2009, 2010 | Marathon seat covers (aftermarket) | Equipment recall: certain Superhides seat covers were not compatible with seat-mounted side air bags and could interfere with deployment. | 2010-09-03 | [S409][S410] |
| 10V115000 | 2010 | Air bag and steering-shaft bracket fasteners | On 2010 vehicles built February 22-March 13, 2010, fasteners for the passenger front air bag module and the steering shaft positioning bracket might not have been torqued; dealers inspected and tightened or replaced them. | 2010-03-24 | [S410] |
| 10V208000 | 2010 | Lower control link welds | On 2010 vehicles built November 19, 2009-March 3, 2010, the lower control link collars could have welds below strength specification; dealers repaired the vehicles. | 2010-05-14 | [S410] |
| 11V592000 | 2011, 2012 | Oil cooler / oil filter connector bolt | Some bolts connecting the engine oil cooler and oil filter to the engine were below strength and could break at the filter, causing an oil leak; dealers replaced the bolts. | 2011-12-19 | [S411][S412] |
| 12V462000 | 2012 | Front wheel hubs (2WD) | 2WD vehicles built June 19-July 12, 2012 could have front hubs below hardness specification that might crack; dealers replaced both front hub assemblies. | 2012-09-20 | [S412] |

No campaigns were returned for 2013, 2014 or 2015 [S413][S414][S415].
