# 2012 Nissan Xterra

[← 2011](2011.md) · [Encyclopedia home](../index.md) · [All years](../index.md#model-years) · [2013 →](2013.md)

> New colors Metallic Blue and Brilliant Silver. Assembly moves from Smyrna, Tennessee to Canton, Mississippi in August 2012. [S8][S24]

## At a glance

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Generation | Second generation, model code N50 [S20] |
| Trims | X, S, PRO-4X [S8] |
| Engine | 4.0-litre DOHC V6, VQ40DE [S20][S112]: 261 hp at 5,600 rpm and 281 lb-ft at 4,000 rpm [S8] |
| Transmissions | 6-speed manual or 5-speed automatic [S8] |
| Drive | 2WD, or part-time 4WD with 2WD/4HI/4LO and an electronically controlled transfer case [S8] |
| Availability | X and S as 2WD or 4WD, PRO-4X 4WD only; X automatic only; 6-speed manual only on S 4x4 and PRO-4X [S8] |
| Built in | Smyrna, Tennessee, until production moved to Canton, Mississippi in August 2012 [S24]; the 2012 kit names Smyrna [S8] |
| Towing | 5,000 lb maximum, 500 lb tongue load, GCWR 9,658 lb [S112] |
| Fuel tank | 21-1/8 US gal (80 L) [S112] |

## What's new for 2012

- New exterior colors Metallic Blue and Brilliant Silver [S8]
- X (steel wheels) and S (alloy wheels) both on P265/70R16 BFGoodrich Long Trail tires [S8]
- The PRO-4X lists the roof-rack gearbox and machine-finished wheels as standard [S8]
- Assembly moved from Smyrna, Tennessee to Canton, Mississippi in August 2012 [S24]

## Trims and equipment

- S: gear basket (4x4) and first-aid kit standard [S8]
- PRO-4X: roof-rack off-road lights, gray/red cloth, P265/75R16 BFGoodrich Rugged Trail T/A tires, Hill Descent Control and Hill Start Assist (automatic only), electronic locking rear differential, Bilstein shocks, skid plates [S8]
- Option: PRO-4X Leather Package (automatic only) [S8]

See [Trim levels](../trims.md) for every year side by side.

## Maintenance schedule (Nissan)

| Item | Interval | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Engine oil and filter | Replace every 7,500 mi / 6 mo (standard); every 3,750 mi / 3 mo (severe) | [S212] |
| Engine air filter | Replace every 30,000 mi / 24 mo | [S212] |
| In-cabin microfilter | S and PRO-4X: replace every 15,000 mi / 12 mo; X: maintenance not required | [S212] |
| Spark plugs | Replace every 105,000 mi / 84 mo | [S212] |
| Engine coolant | First replacement at 105,000 mi / 84 mo, then every 75,000 mi / 60 mo | [S212] |
| Automatic transmission fluid | Inspect every 15,000 mi / 12 mo (standard); severe schedule lists start 15,000 mi / 12 mo, inspect 15,000 mi / 12 mo, replace 15,000 mi / 12 mo | [S212] |
| Manual transmission oil | Inspect every 15,000 mi / 12 mo (standard); severe schedule lists start 15,000 mi / 12 mo, inspect 15,000 mi / 12 mo, replace 15,000 mi / 12 mo | [S212] |
| Transfer case oil (4WD) | Inspect every 15,000 mi / 12 mo (standard); severe schedule lists start 15,000 mi / 12 mo, inspect 15,000 mi / 12 mo, replace 15,000 mi / 12 mo | [S212] |
| Differential oil | Inspect every 15,000 mi / 12 mo (standard); severe schedule lists start 15,000 mi / 12 mo, inspect 15,000 mi / 12 mo, replace 15,000 mi / 12 mo | [S212] |
| Brake fluid | Replace every 30,000 mi / 24 mo (standard); 15,000 mi / 12 mo (severe) | [S212] |
| Tires | Rotate every 7,500 mi | [S212] |
| Drive belts | Inspect from 60,000 mi / 48 mo, then every 15,000 mi / 12 mo | [S212] |

Severe service, per the owner's manual, covers repeated short trips in cold weather, dusty roads, extensive idling, towing and stop-and-go commuting [S115]. How Nissan's 'start / inspect / replace' sequences read is explained on [Maintenance schedule](../maintenance-schedule.md).

## Recalls (NHTSA)

| Campaign | Component | What and remedy | Filed | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 11V592000 | Oil cooler / oil filter connector bolt | Some bolts connecting the engine oil cooler and oil filter to the engine were below strength and could break at the filter, causing an oil leak; dealers replaced the bolts. | 2011-12-19 | [S412] |
| 12V462000 | Front wheel hubs (2WD) | 2WD vehicles built June 19-July 12, 2012 could have front hubs below hardness specification that might crack; dealers replaced both front hub assemblies. | 2012-09-20 | [S412] |

Campaigns filed by Valley Industries, Hopkins, Continental or Marathon cover aftermarket or supplier equipment, and 10E019000 covers replacement parts only, so they do not apply to every vehicle [S412]. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether a campaign applies and was completed. Details: [Recalls](../recalls.md).
