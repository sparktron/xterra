## Towing

| Item | Value | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Maximum towing capacity | 5,000 lb (2,268 kg) | [S108][S109][S110][S111][S112][S113][S114][S115] |
| Maximum tongue load | 500 lb (227 kg) | [S108][S109][S110][S111][S112][S113][S114][S115] |
| Gross combined weight rating | 9,658 lb (4,380 kg) | [S108][S109][S110][S111][S112][S113][S114][S115] |
| Maximum trailer frontal area | 60 sq ft (5.52 m²) | [S115] |
| Trailer brakes required | at 3,500 lb (1,587 kg) and above | [S108] |

Tongue weight should be 10-15% of trailer weight [S115]. The 2015 manual says the capacity assumes a base vehicle with only a driver, and that passengers, cargo and options reduce it [S115]. Nissan does not host manuals before 2008, so the towing rating for 2005-2007 is [Unverified] here.
