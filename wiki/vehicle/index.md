## The generation in brief

- US model years 2005 through 2015 [S1][S11]; EPA lists no 2016 Xterra [S316]
- Body-on-frame: a modified version of the F-Alpha platform developed for the Titan and Armada, with a fully boxed steel ladder frame [S1]
- One engine, the 4.0-litre DOHC VQ40DE V6 [S20][S108]: 265 hp and 284 lb-ft as quoted for 2005-2006 [S1][S2], 261 hp and 281 lb-ft from 2007 [S3][S11]
- Transmissions: 6-speed manual or 5-speed automatic; 2WD or part-time 4WD with 2WD/4HI/4LO [S1][S2][S3][S4][S5][S6][S7][S8][S9][S10][S11]
- Built in Smyrna, Tennessee with engines from Decherd, Tennessee [S1]; production moved to Canton, Mississippi in August 2012 [S24] and ended there in 2015 [S25]
- Nissan introduced the Xterra as a model year 2000 vehicle and had sold more than 750,000 when it announced the end of production [S25]
- The name returns: in April 2026 Nissan confirmed an all-new, US-built Xterra with launch targeted for late 2028 [S26]

## How this section is kept honest

- These pages are written by hand. `xw export` never deletes or rewrites anything under `wiki/vehicle/`; it only links this hub from the top of the wiki index.
- `xw qa` checks every page here: a line that states a number must cite a source listed at the bottom of that page, or say [Unverified]. It checks traceability, not truth; a citation still has to say what the line says.
- Prefer manufacturer sources (press kits, owner's manuals, Nissan's maintenance site), then government data (EPA, NHTSA), then catalogs. Say which when a source is third-party.
- Torque, capacity and part-number values drive real repairs. Confirm part numbers by VIN with a Nissan parts counter, and torque values against the factory service manual for your year.
