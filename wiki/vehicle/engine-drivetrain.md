The 5-speed automatic was derived from the Titan and Armada transmission, the 6-speed manual from a 350Z design [S1]. The online kits for 2005-2011 carry no ratio tables, so these ratios are confirmed only for the years that publish them [Unverified] for earlier years.

## 4WD and traction

- Part-time 4WD with 2WD, 4HI and 4LO, electronically controlled transfer case [S1][S2][S3][S4][S5][S6][S7][S8][S9][S10][S11]
- Transfer case ratios 1.000 (high) and 2.625 (low); the 2015 kit's table prints the two labels the other way round [S11]
- Electronic locking rear differential, Hill Descent Control and Hill Start Assist on the Off-Road and later PRO-4X (HDC/HSA tied to the automatic in the 2008 and later kits) [S4][S7][S11]
- Brake limited-slip differential listed for PRO-4X [S11]
- Vehicle Dynamic Control: available in 2005 [S1], standard from 2006 [S2]
