## What each trim was

- **X** (2006-2015): entry trim, added for 2006 [S2]. Power windows, locks and mirrors, keyless entry and cruise became standard for 2008 [S4]. Steel 16-inch wheels from 2009 [S5][S11]; automatic only from 2011 [S7][S11].
- **S** (2005-2015): the volume trim. Power Package standard from 2006 [S2]; alloy wheels, lumbar support and roof rack cross bars by 2007 [S3]; X Gear content (fog lights, crossbars, gear basket) standard from 2010 [S6]; manual transmission dropped for 2010 [S6], back on the S 4x4 from 2011 [S7].
- **Off-Road** (2005-2010): the off-road trim. By 2007 it had 265/75R16 BFGoodrich Rugged Trail T/A tires, Hill Descent Control, hill start assist, a locking rear differential, Bilstein shocks and skid plates [S3]; 4WD only from 2007 [S3]; roof-rack off-road lights from 2009 [S5]. Renamed PRO-4X for 2011 [S7].
- **SE** (2005-2010): the comfort trim with 17-inch wheels and 265/65R17 tires [S3][S4], automatic only from 2006 [S2], leather standard from 2009 [S5]. Dropped for 2011 [S7].
- **PRO-4X** (2011-2015): the renamed Off-Road [S7]. Bilstein shocks, electronic locking rear differential, Hill Descent Control and Hill Start Assist (automatic only), skid plates for oil pan, fuel tank and transfer case, roof-rack off-road lights [S7][S11]; navigation and RearView Monitor from 2013 [S9]; heated front seats from 2014 [S10].
