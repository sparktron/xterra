The 2015 owner's manual gives the factory-fill coolant a life of 105,000 miles or 7 years [S115]. A 'maintenance not required' or missing entry on Nissan's site is reported as found, not as advice to skip brake-fluid service [Unverified] that it reflects the printed guide.

## Reading Nissan's sequences

For fluids that are inspected and later replaced, Nissan's site lists a sequence such as 'start 15,000 mi, inspect 15,000 mi, replace 15,000 mi' [S212]. Read as successive steps, that is inspect at the first step and replace at the next, but the site does not spell this out [Unverified].

## Severe driving

Use the severe column if you often make short trips in cold weather, drive on dusty roads, idle a lot, tow, or commute in stop-and-go traffic [S115]. Towing also calls for more frequent final-drive oil service [S115].
