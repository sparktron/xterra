# Fluids and capacities

[Encyclopedia home](index.md)

From the 'Recommended fluids/lubricants and capacities' tables of the 2008-2015 owner's manuals [S108][S109][S110][S111][S112][S113][S114][S115]. Nissan does not host owner's manuals before 2008, so 2005-2007 values are [Unverified]; the 2008 values are the closest documented. The manuals call capacities approximate: fill to the dipstick or level mark.

## What changed between manuals

| Item | Years | Specification | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Automatic transmission fluid | 2008 | Genuine Nissan Matic J ATF | [S108] |
| Automatic transmission fluid | 2009-2015 | Genuine Nissan Matic S ATF; Matic J if S is unavailable | [S109][S110][S111][S112][S113][S114][S115] |
| Coolant mix | 2008-2010 | 50% Long Life coolant, 50% demineralized or distilled water | [S108][S109][S110] |
| Coolant mix | 2011-2015 | pre-diluted Long Life coolant (blue) | [S111][S112][S113][S114][S115] |
| Manual transmission | 2008-2009 | 2WD 4-1/4 qt (3.98 L), 4WD 4-3/8 qt (4.18 L); MTF HQ Multi 75W-85 or API GL-4 75W-85 or 75W-90 | [S108][S109] |
| Manual transmission | 2010-2013 | 2WD 4-1/4 qt (4.0 L), 4WD 4-3/8 qt (4.2 L); MTF HQ Multi 75W-85 or API GL-4 75W-85 | [S110][S111][S112][S113] |
| Manual transmission | 2014-2015 | 4WD 4-3/8 qt (4.2 L); MTF HQ Multi 75W-85 or API GL-4 75W-85 | [S114][S115] |
| Transfer case capacity | 2010-2015 | 2-1/8 qt (2.0 L) | [S110][S111][S112][S113][S114][S115] |
| Front differential capacity | 2010-2015 | 1-3/4 pt (0.85 L) | [S110][S111][S112][S113][S114][S115] |
| Rear differential | 2008 | synthetic GL-5 75W-90; 6-speed manual and 5-speed automatic 4x4 Off-Road: synthetic 75W-140 | [S108] |
| Rear differential | 2009 | synthetic GL-5 75W-90 (no capacity listed) | [S109] |
| Rear differential | 2010-2011 | with A/T 3-3/8 pt (1.6 L), with M/T 4-1/4 pt (2.0 L); synthetic GL-5 75W-90 | [S110][S111] |
| Rear differential | 2012-2013 | with A/T 3-3/8 pt (1.6 L) synthetic 75W-90; with M/T 4-1/4 pt (2.0 L) synthetic 75W-140 | [S112][S113] |
| Rear differential | 2014-2015 | A/T without E-Lock 3-3/8 pt (1.6 L) synthetic 75W-90; M/T or A/T with E-Lock 4-1/4 pt (2.0 L) synthetic 75W-140 | [S114][S115] |
| Oil drain plug torque | 2008-2014 | 22-29 ft-lb (29-39 N·m) | [S108][S109][S110][S111][S112][S113][S114] |
| Oil drain plug torque | 2015 | 25 ft-lb (34 N·m) | [S115] |

The rear-differential wording differs between manuals. The 2014-2015 manuals tie the 75W-140 fill to the electronic locker (E-Lock) [S114][S115], while the 2010-2013 manuals split by transmission [S110][S111][S112][S113]. Before buying gear oil, check which axle you have and what your own manual says.

A/C compressor oil: the capacities table names Nissan A/C System Oil Type R [S115], while the 2015 manual's A/C section names Type S [S115]. Check the A/C specification label under the hood [S115].
