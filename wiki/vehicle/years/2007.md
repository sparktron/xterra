## What's new for 2007

- Nissan's kit now quotes 261 hp and 281 lb-ft (the 2005 and 2006 kits quoted 265 hp and 284 lb-ft) [S3]
- Mud guards standard on all 4x4 models [S3]
- An auxiliary input jack joins the Rockford Fosgate Audio Package on the SE [S3]
- Off-Road gets a standard leather-wrapped steering wheel and sunglass holder [S3]
- Body side molding standard on the SE [S3]
- New exterior color Desert Stone [S3]

## Trims and equipment

- X: VDC, 4-wheel disc brakes with ABS, 60/40 fold-flat second row, Easy Clean cargo surface, C-channel cargo floor with four movable hooks [S3]
- S adds aluminum-alloy wheels, driver's lumbar support, roof rack cross bars and the Power Package content [S3]
- Off-Road: 265/75R16 BFGoodrich Rugged Trail T/A tires, Hill Descent Control and hill start assist, locking rear differential, Bilstein shocks, skid plates, leather steering wheel [S3]
- SE: 17-inch alloy wheels, trip computer, step rails, fog lamps, Rockford Fosgate audio with in-dash 6-disc changer, eight speakers plus subwoofer [S3]
- Packages: Utility, Power, Rockford Fosgate Audio, Satellite Radio (XM or Sirius), Side/Curtain Air Bag [S3]

## Maintenance schedule (Nissan)

| Item | Interval | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Engine oil and filter | Replace every 7,500 mi / 6 mo (standard); every 3,750 mi / 3 mo (severe) | [S207] |
| Engine air filter | Replace every 30,000 mi / 24 mo | [S207] |
| In-cabin microfilter | Replace every 15,000 mi / 12 mo | [S207] |
| Spark plugs | Replace every 105,000 mi / 84 mo | [S207] |
| Engine coolant | First replacement at 60,000 mi / 48 mo, then every 30,000 mi / 24 mo | [S207] |
| Automatic transmission fluid | Inspect every 15,000 mi / 12 mo (standard); severe schedule lists start 15,000 mi / 12 mo, inspect 15,000 mi / 12 mo, replace 15,000 mi / 12 mo | [S207] |
| Manual transmission oil | Inspect every 15,000 mi / 12 mo (standard); severe schedule lists start 15,000 mi / 12 mo, inspect 15,000 mi / 12 mo, replace 15,000 mi / 12 mo | [S207] |
| Transfer case oil (4WD) | Inspect every 15,000 mi / 12 mo (standard); severe schedule lists start 15,000 mi / 12 mo, inspect 15,000 mi / 12 mo, replace 15,000 mi / 12 mo | [S207] |
| Differential oil | Inspect every 15,000 mi / 12 mo (standard); severe schedule lists start 15,000 mi / 12 mo, inspect 15,000 mi / 12 mo, replace 15,000 mi / 12 mo | [S207] |
| Brake fluid | Listed as 'maintenance not required' | [S207] |
| Tires | Rotate every 7,500 mi | [S207] |
| Drive belts | Inspect from 60,000 mi / 48 mo, then every 15,000 mi / 12 mo | [S207] |

Severe service, per the owner's manual, covers repeated short trips in cold weather, dusty roads, extensive idling, towing and stop-and-go commuting [S115]. How Nissan's 'start / inspect / replace' sequences read is explained on [Maintenance schedule](../maintenance-schedule.md).

## Recalls (NHTSA)

| Campaign | Component | What and remedy | Filed | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 10E019000 | Replacement lower control links | Front and rear lower links sold as replacement parts between November 25, 2009 and May 14, 2010 could have weak collar welds; dealers replaced affected links. | 2010-05-20 | [S407] |
| 08V690000 | Front crash zone sensor (salt states) | In heavy road-salt states, salt water could enter the front crash zone sensor housing and corrode it, lighting the air bag warning lamp and risking non-deployment of the front air bags; dealers fitted a redesigned sensor, and owners elsewhere got an extended sensor warranty. | 2008-12-29 | [S407] |

Campaigns filed by Valley Industries, Hopkins, Continental or Marathon cover aftermarket or supplier equipment, and 10E019000 covers replacement parts only, so they do not apply to every vehicle [S407].
