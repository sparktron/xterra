# 2015 Nissan Xterra

[← 2014](2014.md) · [Encyclopedia home](../index.md) · [All years](../index.md#model-years) · Last model year

> Final model year of the Xterra (production at Canton ended in 2015). The S gains NissanConnect with Mobile Apps. [S11][S25]

## At a glance

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Generation | Second generation, model code N50 [S20] |
| Trims | X, S, PRO-4X [S11] |
| Engine | 4.0-litre DOHC V6, VQ40DE [S20][S115]: 261 hp at 5,600 rpm and 281 lb-ft at 4,000 rpm [S11] |
| Transmissions | 6-speed manual or 5-speed automatic [S11] |
| Drive | 2WD, or part-time 4WD with 2WD/4HI/4LO and an electronically controlled transfer case [S11] |
| Availability | X and S as 2WD or 4WD, PRO-4X 4WD only; X automatic only; 6-speed manual only on S 4x4 and PRO-4X [S11] |
| Built in | Canton, Mississippi [S24][S25]; the 2015 press kit's boilerplate still names Smyrna and Decherd, Tennessee [S11], which conflicts with the August 2012 move [S24] |
| Towing | 5,000 lb maximum, 500 lb tongue load, GCWR 9,658 lb [S115] |
| Fuel tank | 21-1/8 US gal (80 L) [S115] |

## What's new for 2015

- S adds NissanConnect with Mobile Apps, a 5.0-inch color display, USB port, Bluetooth audio streaming, a hands-free text messaging assistant and SiriusXM [S11]
- Last model year: Nissan announced on August 18, 2015 that Xterra production at Canton would stop, with the last units sold in fall 2015 [S25]; EPA's 2016 Nissan model list has no Xterra [S316]

## Trims and equipment

- X: 16-inch steel wheels with 265/70R16 BFGoodrich Long Trail tires, automatic only, 4-way driver's seat [S11]
- S: 16-inch alloys with 265/70R16 BFGoodrich Long Trail tires, 8-way driver's seat with lumbar, power mirrors, cargo gear basket (4x4), first-aid kit, front tow hook (4x4) [S11]
- PRO-4X: roof-rack off-road lights, auto headlights, RearView Monitor, NissanConnect with Navigation (5.8-inch touch screen), 300-watt Rockford Fosgate audio, heated cloth seats, 265/75R16 all-terrain tires, Hill Descent Control and Hill Start Assist (automatic only), electronic locking rear differential, Bilstein shocks, skid plates [S11]
- Option: PRO-4X Leather Package (automatic only) [S11]

See [Trim levels](../trims.md) for every year side by side.

## Maintenance schedule (Nissan)

| Item | Interval | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Engine oil and filter | Replace every 5,000 mi / 6 mo (standard and severe) | [S215] |
| Engine air filter | Replace every 30,000 mi / 36 mo | [S215] |
| In-cabin microfilter | S and PRO-4X: replace every 15,000 mi / 18 mo; X: maintenance not required | [S215] |
| Spark plugs | Replace every 105,000 mi / 126 mo | [S215] |
| Engine coolant | First replacement at 105,000 mi / 84 mo, then every 75,000 mi / 60 mo | [S215] |
| Automatic transmission fluid | Inspect every 10,000 mi / 12 mo (standard); severe schedule lists start 10,000 mi / 12 mo, inspect 10,000 mi / 12 mo, replace 10,000 mi / 12 mo | [S215] |
| Manual transmission oil | Inspect every 10,000 mi / 12 mo (standard); severe schedule lists start 10,000 mi / 12 mo, inspect 10,000 mi / 12 mo, replace 10,000 mi / 12 mo | [S215] |
| Transfer case oil (4WD) | Inspect every 10,000 mi / 12 mo (standard); severe schedule lists start 10,000 mi / 12 mo, inspect 10,000 mi / 12 mo, replace 10,000 mi / 12 mo | [S215] |
| Differential oil | Inspect every 10,000 mi / 12 mo (standard); severe schedule lists start 10,000 mi / 12 mo, inspect 10,000 mi / 12 mo, replace 10,000 mi / 12 mo | [S215] |
| Brake fluid | Replace every 20,000 mi / 24 mo (standard); 10,000 mi / 12 mo (severe) | [S215] |
| Tires | Rotate every 5,000 mi | [S215] |
| Drive belts | Inspect from 40,000 mi / 48 mo, then every 10,000 mi / 12 mo | [S215] |

Severe service, per the owner's manual, covers repeated short trips in cold weather, dusty roads, extensive idling, towing and stop-and-go commuting [S115]. How Nissan's 'start / inspect / replace' sequences read is explained on [Maintenance schedule](../maintenance-schedule.md).
