> Second year. A new entry-level X trim arrives, the S gets the Power Package as standard, and VDC becomes standard. [S2]

## What's new for 2006

- New entry-level X trim alongside the S, Off-Road and SE [S2]
- Power Package (immobilizer and alarm, remote keyless entry, power locks, mirrors and windows, cruise control) standard on the S and available on the X [S2]
- Glove box gains a lock, lamp and damper on every trim [S2]
- Factory satellite radio available, with a choice of XM or Sirius [S2]
- Vehicle Dynamic Control (VDC) listed as standard [S2]
- New exterior color Midnight Blue; new Charcoal interior for the Off-Road [S2]
- Output still quoted at 265 hp and 284 lb-ft, rated to SAE J1349 JUN1995 [S2]

## Trims and equipment

- Packages: Utility (step rails, illuminated visor mirrors, cargo net, flat-fold passenger seat, first-aid kit), Rockford Fosgate Audio (in-dash 6-disc changer, MP3, eight speakers plus subwoofer, steering-wheel controls), Satellite Radio, and Side/Curtain Air Bag [S2]
- Off-Road seat fabric in blue or charcoal [S2]

## Recalls (NHTSA)

| Campaign | Component | What and remedy | Filed | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 10E019000 | Replacement lower control links | Front and rear lower links sold as replacement parts between November 25, 2009 and May 14, 2010 could have weak collar welds; dealers replaced affected links. | 2010-05-20 | [S406] |
| 10V517000 | IPDM ECM relay | The ECM relay inside the intelligent power distribution module (IPDM) could develop silicon oxide on its contacts and cause stalling; dealers replaced the relay. | 2010-10-28 | [S406] |
| 08V690000 | Front crash zone sensor (salt states) | In heavy road-salt states, salt water could enter the front crash zone sensor housing and corrode it, lighting the air bag warning lamp and risking non-deployment of the front air bags; dealers fitted a redesigned sensor, and owners elsewhere got an extended sensor warranty. | 2008-12-29 | [S406] |
| 07E046000 | Hopkins trailer brake harness (aftermarket) | Equipment recall: some Hopkins trailer-brake connection harnesses had a wire in the wrong connector position, sending brake-controller output to the dash-light circuit instead of the trailer brakes; Hopkins replaced the harness. | 2007-07-03 | [S406] |
| 10V075000 | Fuel level sender | On some 2006 and 2008 vehicles the molded fuel tank shell could deform so the gauge read about a quarter tank when empty; dealers replaced the sending unit with one having a modified float arm. | 2010-03-03 | [S406] |

Campaigns filed by Valley Industries, Hopkins, Continental or Marathon cover aftermarket or supplier equipment, and 10E019000 covers replacement parts only, so they do not apply to every vehicle [S406]. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether a campaign applies and was completed. Details: [Recalls](../recalls.md).
