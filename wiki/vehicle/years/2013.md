# 2013 Nissan Xterra

[← 2012](2012.md) · [Encyclopedia home](../index.md) · [All years](../index.md#model-years) · [2014 →](2014.md)

> The PRO-4X gains navigation, a rearview monitor and auto headlights; the S gets Display Audio and SiriusXM. Glacier White and Cayenne Red arrive. [S9]

## At a glance

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Generation | Second generation, model code N50 [S20] |
| Trims | X, S, PRO-4X [S9] |
| Engine | 4.0-litre DOHC V6, VQ40DE [S20][S113]: 261 hp at 5,600 rpm and 281 lb-ft at 4,000 rpm [S9] |
| Transmissions | 6-speed manual or 5-speed automatic [S9] |
| Drive | 2WD, or part-time 4WD with 2WD/4HI/4LO and an electronically controlled transfer case [S9] |
| Availability | X and S as 2WD or 4WD, PRO-4X 4WD only; X automatic only; 6-speed manual only on S 4x4 and PRO-4X [S9] |
| Built in | Canton, Mississippi [S24]; the 2013 press kit's boilerplate still names Smyrna and Decherd, Tennessee [S9], which conflicts with the August 2012 move [S24] |
| Towing | 5,000 lb maximum, 500 lb tongue load, GCWR 9,658 lb [S113] |
| Fuel tank | 21-1/8 US gal (80 L) [S113] |

## What's new for 2013

- PRO-4X adds updated contrast stitching and seat embroidery, auto on/off headlights, an outside temperature display, RearView Monitor and navigation [S9]
- S adds Display Audio (CD/AM/FM/aux/USB) and SiriusXM satellite radio [S9]
- Slide-on-rod sun visors on every trim [S9]
- New exterior colors Glacier White and Cayenne Red; a PRO-4X graphics package becomes available [S9]

## Trims and equipment

- PRO-4X: gray/white cloth, 265/75R16 BFGoodrich Rugged Trail T/A tires, Hill Descent Control and Hill Start Assist (automatic only), electronic locking rear differential, Bilstein shocks, skid plates [S9]
- Option: PRO-4X Leather Package [S9]

See [Trim levels](../trims.md) for every year side by side.

## Maintenance schedule (Nissan)

| Item | Interval | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Engine oil and filter | Replace every 7,500 mi / 6 mo (standard); every 3,750 mi / 3 mo (severe) | [S213] |
| Engine air filter | Replace every 30,000 mi / 24 mo | [S213] |
| In-cabin microfilter | S and PRO-4X: replace every 15,000 mi / 12 mo; X: maintenance not required | [S213] |
| Spark plugs | Replace every 105,000 mi / 84 mo | [S213] |
| Engine coolant | First replacement at 105,000 mi / 84 mo, then every 75,000 mi / 60 mo | [S213] |
| Automatic transmission fluid | Inspect every 15,000 mi / 12 mo (standard); severe schedule lists start 15,000 mi / 12 mo, inspect 15,000 mi / 12 mo, replace 15,000 mi / 12 mo | [S213] |
| Manual transmission oil | Inspect every 15,000 mi / 12 mo (standard); severe schedule lists start 15,000 mi / 12 mo, inspect 15,000 mi / 12 mo, replace 15,000 mi / 12 mo | [S213] |
| Transfer case oil (4WD) | Inspect every 15,000 mi / 12 mo (standard); severe schedule lists start 15,000 mi / 12 mo, inspect 15,000 mi / 12 mo, replace 15,000 mi / 12 mo | [S213] |
| Differential oil | Inspect every 15,000 mi / 12 mo (standard); severe schedule lists start 15,000 mi / 12 mo, inspect 15,000 mi / 12 mo, replace 15,000 mi / 12 mo | [S213] |
| Brake fluid | Replace every 30,000 mi / 24 mo (standard); 15,000 mi / 12 mo (severe) | [S213] |
| Tires | Rotate every 7,500 mi | [S213] |
| Drive belts | Inspect from 60,000 mi / 48 mo, then every 15,000 mi / 12 mo | [S213] |

Severe service, per the owner's manual, covers repeated short trips in cold weather, dusty roads, extensive idling, towing and stop-and-go commuting [S115]. How Nissan's 'start / inspect / replace' sequences read is explained on [Maintenance schedule](../maintenance-schedule.md).
