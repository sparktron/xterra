# 2011 Nissan Xterra

> The Off-Road becomes the PRO-4X and the SE is dropped, leaving X, S and PRO-4X. Lava Red and Metallic Green are new. [S7]

## What's new for 2011

- The Off-Road model is renamed PRO-4X and the SE is discontinued [S7]
- Body side moldings added to the S; first-aid kit and rear tire deflector added to the S 4x2; white-faced gauges on the PRO-4X [S7]
- New exterior colors Lava Red and Metallic Green [S7]
- One option: the PRO-4X Leather Package (automatic only) with Graphite leather-appointed seats and a passenger seatback pocket [S7]

## Trims and equipment

- S: 16-inch alloys with P265/70R16 BFGoodrich Long Trail tires, step rails, roof rack crossbars and gear basket (4x4), first-aid kit [S7]
- PRO-4X: roof-rack off-road lights, gray/red cloth, P265/75R16 BFGoodrich Rugged Trail T/A tires, Hill Descent Control and Hill Start Assist (automatic only), electronic locking rear differential, Bilstein shocks, skid plates, Rockford Fosgate audio, XM and Bluetooth [S7]

## Maintenance schedule (Nissan)

| Item | Interval | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Engine oil and filter | Replace every 7,500 mi / 6 mo (standard); every 3,750 mi / 3 mo (severe) | [S211] |
| Engine air filter | Replace every 30,000 mi / 24 mo | [S211] |
| In-cabin microfilter | S and PRO-4X: replace every 15,000 mi / 12 mo; X: maintenance not required | [S211] |
| Spark plugs | Replace every 105,000 mi / 84 mo | [S211] |
| Engine coolant | First replacement at 105,000 mi / 84 mo, then every 75,000 mi / 60 mo | [S211] |
| Automatic transmission fluid | Inspect every 15,000 mi / 12 mo (standard); severe schedule lists start 15,000 mi / 12 mo, inspect 15,000 mi / 12 mo, replace 15,000 mi / 12 mo | [S211] |
| Manual transmission oil | Inspect every 15,000 mi / 12 mo (standard); severe schedule lists start 15,000 mi / 12 mo, inspect 15,000 mi / 12 mo, replace 15,000 mi / 12 mo | [S211] |
| Transfer case oil (4WD) | Inspect every 15,000 mi / 12 mo (standard); severe schedule lists start 15,000 mi / 12 mo, inspect 15,000 mi / 12 mo, replace 15,000 mi / 12 mo | [S211] |
| Differential oil | Inspect every 15,000 mi / 12 mo (standard); severe schedule lists start 15,000 mi / 12 mo, inspect 15,000 mi / 12 mo, replace 15,000 mi / 12 mo | [S211] |
| Brake fluid | Replace every 30,000 mi / 24 mo (standard); 15,000 mi / 12 mo (severe) | [S211] |
| Tires | Rotate every 7,500 mi | [S211] |
| Drive belts | Inspect from 60,000 mi / 48 mo, then every 15,000 mi / 12 mo | [S211] |

Severe service, per the owner's manual, covers repeated short trips in cold weather, dusty roads, extensive idling, towing and stop-and-go commuting [S115]. How Nissan's 'start / inspect / replace' sequences read is explained on [Maintenance schedule](../maintenance-schedule.md).

|---|---|---|---|---|
