# 2005 Nissan Xterra

First year of this generation · [Encyclopedia home](../index.md) · [All years](../index.md#model-years) · [2006 →](2006.md)

## What's new for 2005

- Rides on a modified version of the F-Alpha platform developed for the Titan pickup and Armada, with a fully boxed all-steel ladder frame [S1]
- New 4.0-litre DOHC V6, a longer-stroke member of the VQ family, rated at 265 hp and 284 lb-ft [S1]
- 5-speed automatic derived from the Titan/Armada unit; 6-speed manual derived from a 350Z design [S1]
- Wheelbase 2.0 in longer than the first generation while overall length grew only 0.2 in; width went from 70.4 to 72.8 in and height from 74.4 to 74.9 in [S1]
- Front legroom 41.4 to 42.4 in and front headroom 38.6 to 39.9 in; second-row legroom up 1.6 in, headroom up 1.5 in, shoulder room up 3.9 in [S1]
- Roof rack gains a latchable lid; the rear bumper has side steps for reaching the rack [S1]
- Cargo area: Easy Clean floor, 10 utility hooks (six floor/side, four ceiling; floor hooks rated to 110 lb), an adjustable floor channel system like the Titan's Utili-track, and an available first-aid kit [S1]
- Available 300-watt Rockford Fosgate audio with nine speakers and MP3 playback; satellite radio needed a separately bought receiver and antenna [S1]

## Trims and equipment

- Part-time 4WD with 2WD/4HI/4LO modes and an electronically controlled transfer case; VDC available [S1]
- Double-wishbone front suspension with stabilizer bar and solid-axle leaf-spring rear; high-performance gas shocks available on the Off-Road [S1]
- 4-wheel disc brakes with ABS and EBD; engine-speed-sensitive rack-and-pinion steering; wheels up to 17-inch with 265/65R17 tires [S1]
- Off-Road has its own blue seat fabric; a fold-down front passenger seat is available [S1]
- Nissan Advanced Air Bag System with occupant classification sensor; front side and roof-mounted curtain air bags were available, not standard [S1]
- Spark plugs rated for 105,000 miles [S1]
