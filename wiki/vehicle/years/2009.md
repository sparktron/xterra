## What's new for 2009

- Reshaped grille and front bumper fascia: Sandblast Aluminum grille on X, S and Off-Road, Satin Chrome on SE; black headlight surrounds on X and S [S5]
- Off-Road gets roof-mounted off-road lights built into the roof-rack air dam [S5]
- New wheel designs on every trim, including a 16-inch bright machine-finished 6-spoke wheel for the Off-Road [S5]
- New center instrument panel fascia and HVAC switches; new seat cloths; leather seating standard on the SE [S5]
- Roof-mounted curtain air bags with rollover sensor become standard; TPMS and front seat active head restraints listed as standard [S5]
- New 4x4 tailgate badge; new side-step and roof-rail color (Sandblast Aluminum) [S5]
- Two new exterior colors, Navy Blue and Red Brick; the at-a-glance sheet counts nine colors in total [S5]
- Options cut to two packages, Technology (S, Off-Road, SE) and X Gear (S, SE: roof crossbars, fog lights, first-aid kit, cargo net, gear basket); Technology Package content was free on S, Off-Road and SE for a limited time from the July 2008 launch [S5]

## Trims and equipment

- X: 16-inch steel wheels with P265/70R16 tires, 4-way driver's seat [S5]
- S: 16-inch alloys with P265/70R16 BFGoodrich Long Trail tires, lumbar support, step rails, Easy Clean cargo surface, C-channel floor, front tow hook (4x4), 8-way driver's seat [S5]
- Off-Road: P265/75R16 BFGoodrich Rugged Trail T/A tires, Hill Descent Control and Hill Start Assist, electronic locking rear differential, Bilstein shocks, skid plates, auto-dimming mirror with compass [S5]
- SE: 17-inch alloys with P265/65R17 BFGoodrich Long Trail tires, leather seats, trip computer, body-colored mirrors, handles and molding, XM [S5]

## Maintenance schedule (Nissan)

| Item | Interval | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Engine oil and filter | Replace every 7,500 mi / 6 mo (standard); every 3,750 mi / 3 mo (severe) | [S209] |
| Engine air filter | Replace every 30,000 mi / 24 mo | [S209] |
| In-cabin microfilter | Replace every 15,000 mi / 12 mo | [S209] |
| Spark plugs | Replace every 105,000 mi / 84 mo | [S209] |
| Engine coolant | First replacement at 60,000 mi / 48 mo, then every 30,000 mi / 24 mo | [S209] |
| Automatic transmission fluid | Inspect every 15,000 mi / 12 mo (standard); severe schedule lists start 15,000 mi / 12 mo, inspect 15,000 mi / 12 mo, replace 15,000 mi / 12 mo | [S209] |
| Manual transmission oil | Inspect every 15,000 mi / 12 mo (standard); severe schedule lists start 15,000 mi / 12 mo, inspect 15,000 mi / 12 mo, replace 15,000 mi / 12 mo | [S209] |
| Transfer case oil (4WD) | Inspect every 15,000 mi / 12 mo (standard); severe schedule lists start 15,000 mi / 12 mo, inspect 15,000 mi / 12 mo, replace 15,000 mi / 12 mo | [S209] |
| Differential oil | Inspect every 15,000 mi / 12 mo (standard); severe schedule lists start 15,000 mi / 12 mo, inspect 15,000 mi / 12 mo, replace 15,000 mi / 12 mo | [S209] |
| Brake fluid | Listed as 'maintenance not required' | [S209] |
| Tires | Rotate every 7,500 mi | [S209] |
| Drive belts | Inspect from 60,000 mi / 48 mo, then every 15,000 mi / 12 mo | [S209] |

Severe service, per the owner's manual, covers repeated short trips in cold weather, dusty roads, extensive idling, towing and stop-and-go commuting [S115]. How Nissan's 'start / inspect / replace' sequences read is explained on [Maintenance schedule](../maintenance-schedule.md).

|---|---|---|---|---|
