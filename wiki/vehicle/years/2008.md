## What's new for 2008

- X gains standard power windows, locks and mirrors, remote keyless entry and cruise control [S4]
- S gains standard step rails, first-aid kit and cargo net [S4]
- New Technology Package: Bluetooth hands-free phone, XM satellite radio (pre-wiring only on manual-transmission models), auto-dimming mirror with compass and Rockford Fosgate audio with subwoofer; standard on SE, optional on Off-Road [S4]
- New exterior color Red Alert and new Sandstone interior [S4]

## Trims and equipment

- VDC standard on 4x2 and 4x4 [S4]
- Wheels and tires: 16-inch with 265/70R16 on X and S; 16-inch with 265/75R16 BFGoodrich Rugged Trail T/A on Off-Road; 17-inch with 265/65R17 BFGoodrich Long Trail on SE [S4]
- S: aluminum wheels, lumbar support, roof rack cross bars, gear basket, Easy Clean cargo surface, C-channel floor with four movable hooks [S4]
- Off-Road: fog lamps, black body side molding, fold-flat passenger seat, blue cloth, leather wheel, Hill Descent Control and Hill Start Assist (4WD automatic), electronic locking rear differential, Bilstein gas shocks, rear tire deflector, and skid plates for oil pan, fuel tank and transfer case [S4]
- SE: trip computer, body-colored mirrors, fog lamps, Bluetooth, XM, Rockford Fosgate with 6-CD changer [S4]
- Options: Side/Curtain Air Bag Package; Technology Package (Off-Road only) [S4]

## Maintenance schedule (Nissan)

| Item | Interval | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Engine oil and filter | Replace every 7,500 mi / 6 mo (standard); every 3,750 mi / 3 mo (severe) | [S208] |
| Engine air filter | Replace every 30,000 mi / 24 mo | [S208] |
| In-cabin microfilter | Not listed for this year on Nissan's site | [S208] |
| Spark plugs | Replace every 105,000 mi / 84 mo | [S208] |
| Engine coolant | First replacement at 60,000 mi / 48 mo, then every 30,000 mi / 24 mo | [S208] |
| Automatic transmission fluid | Inspect every 15,000 mi / 12 mo (standard); severe schedule lists start 15,000 mi / 12 mo, inspect 15,000 mi / 12 mo, replace 15,000 mi / 12 mo | [S208] |
| Manual transmission oil | Inspect every 15,000 mi / 12 mo (standard); severe schedule lists start 15,000 mi / 12 mo, inspect 15,000 mi / 12 mo, replace 15,000 mi / 12 mo | [S208] |
| Transfer case oil (4WD) | Inspect every 15,000 mi / 12 mo (standard); severe schedule lists start 15,000 mi / 12 mo, inspect 15,000 mi / 12 mo, replace 15,000 mi / 12 mo | [S208] |
| Differential oil | Inspect every 15,000 mi / 12 mo (standard); severe schedule lists start 15,000 mi / 12 mo, inspect 15,000 mi / 12 mo, replace 15,000 mi / 12 mo | [S208] |
| Brake fluid | Listed as 'maintenance not required' | [S208] |
| Tires | Rotate every 7,500 mi | [S208] |
| Drive belts | Inspect from 60,000 mi / 48 mo, then every 15,000 mi / 12 mo | [S208] |

Severe service, per the owner's manual, covers repeated short trips in cold weather, dusty roads, extensive idling, towing and stop-and-go commuting [S115]. How Nissan's 'start / inspect / replace' sequences read is explained on [Maintenance schedule](../maintenance-schedule.md).
