# 2014 Nissan Xterra

[← 2013](2013.md) · [Encyclopedia home](../index.md) · [All years](../index.md#model-years) · [2015 →](2015.md)

> NissanConnect Apps, new wheel designs, Bluetooth on every trim and heated seats on the PRO-4X. [S10]

## At a glance

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Generation | Second generation, model code N50 [S20] |
| Trims | X, S, PRO-4X [S10] |
| Engine | 4.0-litre DOHC V6, VQ40DE [S20][S114]: 261 hp at 5,600 rpm and 281 lb-ft at 4,000 rpm [S10] |
| Transmissions | 6-speed manual or 5-speed automatic [S10] |
| Drive | 2WD, or part-time 4WD with 2WD/4HI/4LO and an electronically controlled transfer case [S10] |
| Availability | X and S as 2WD or 4WD, PRO-4X 4WD only; X automatic only; 6-speed manual only on S 4x4 and PRO-4X [S10] |
| Built in | Canton, Mississippi [S24]; the 2014 press kit's boilerplate still names Smyrna and Decherd, Tennessee [S10], which conflicts with the August 2012 move [S24] |
| Towing | 5,000 lb maximum, 500 lb tongue load, GCWR 9,658 lb [S114] |
| Fuel tank | 21-1/8 US gal (80 L) [S114] |

## What's new for 2014

- NissanConnect with Navigation adds NissanConnect Apps smartphone integration, SiriusXM Traffic and SiriusXM Travel Link [S10]
- New 16-inch alloy wheel designs for the S and PRO-4X [S10]
- Bluetooth hands-free phone, steering-wheel audio controls and an overhead sunglass holder become standard on every trim [S10]
- X gains push-type HVAC controls and an upgraded audio system [S10]
- PRO-4X gains heated front seats (cloth standard, leather available) [S10]
- Hyper Silver paint for the grille and underguard [S10]

## Trims and equipment

- PRO-4X: RearView Monitor, NissanConnect with Navigation and a 5.8-inch touch screen, Rockford Fosgate audio with CD changer, eight speakers and subwoofer [S10]
- Options: PRO-4X Leather Package; first-aid kit in the X Gear Package [S10]

## Maintenance schedule (Nissan)

| Item | Interval | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Engine oil and filter | Replace every 5,000 mi / 6 mo (standard and severe) | [S214] |
| Engine air filter | Replace every 30,000 mi / 36 mo | [S214] |
| In-cabin microfilter | S and PRO-4X: replace every 15,000 mi / 18 mo; X: maintenance not required | [S214] |
| Spark plugs | Replace every 105,000 mi / 126 mo | [S214] |
| Engine coolant | First replacement at 105,000 mi / 84 mo, then every 75,000 mi / 60 mo | [S214] |
| Automatic transmission fluid | Inspect every 10,000 mi / 12 mo (standard); severe schedule lists start 10,000 mi / 12 mo, inspect 10,000 mi / 12 mo, replace 10,000 mi / 12 mo | [S214] |
| Manual transmission oil | Inspect every 10,000 mi / 12 mo (standard); severe schedule lists start 10,000 mi / 12 mo, inspect 10,000 mi / 12 mo, replace 10,000 mi / 12 mo | [S214] |
| Transfer case oil (4WD) | Inspect every 10,000 mi / 12 mo (standard); severe schedule lists start 10,000 mi / 12 mo, inspect 10,000 mi / 12 mo, replace 10,000 mi / 12 mo | [S214] |
| Differential oil | Inspect every 10,000 mi / 12 mo (standard); severe schedule lists start 10,000 mi / 12 mo, inspect 10,000 mi / 12 mo, replace 10,000 mi / 12 mo | [S214] |
| Brake fluid | Replace every 20,000 mi / 24 mo (standard); 10,000 mi / 12 mo (severe) | [S214] |
| Tires | Rotate every 5,000 mi | [S214] |
| Drive belts | Inspect from 40,000 mi / 48 mo, then every 10,000 mi / 12 mo | [S214] |

Severe service, per the owner's manual, covers repeated short trips in cold weather, dusty roads, extensive idling, towing and stop-and-go commuting [S115]. How Nissan's 'start / inspect / replace' sequences read is explained on [Maintenance schedule](../maintenance-schedule.md).
