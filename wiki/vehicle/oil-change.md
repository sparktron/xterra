Use a new drain plug washer every time. Capacities are approximate; the manual says to finish with the dipstick.

| Model years | Standard driving | Severe driving | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2005-2013 | 7,500 mi / 6 months | 3,750 mi / 3 months | [S205][S206][S207][S208][S209][S210][S211][S212][S213] |
| 2014-2015 | 5,000 mi / 6 months | 5,000 mi / 6 months | [S214][S215] |

Severe use covers repeated short trips in cold weather, dusty roads, extensive idling, towing a trailer and stop-and-go commuting [S115].

## Procedure (paraphrased from the 2015 owner's manual)

1. Park level, set the parking brake, idle the engine to operating temperature, then switch it off [S115].
2. Remove the oil filler cap. Put a large drain pan under the drain plug and remove it to drain the oil completely. [S115]
3. For a filter change, remove the screws holding the oil filter access cover in the under-engine cover. Loosen the filter with a filter wrench, then unscrew it by hand. [S115]
4. Wipe the filter sealing surface clean and remove any old gasket. Coat the new filter's gasket with clean oil. [S115]
5. Screw on the new filter until it seats, then turn it another 2/3 turn. Refit the access cover [S115].
6. Refit the drain plug with a new washer and tighten it to the torque for your manual year (table above) [S115].
7. Refill through the filler opening and refit the cap. [S115]
8. Start the engine and check for leaks at the drain plug and filter. [S115]
9. Stop the engine, wait more than 10 minutes, then check the dipstick and top up [S115].

The dealer is the manual's default recommendation. Hot oil burns, and used oil must be disposed of according to local regulations.
