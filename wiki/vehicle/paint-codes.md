## Where the lists disagree

- **2009**: ImportArchive gives eight colors and ends Solar Yellow (EW3) in 2008 [S20], but Nissan's 2009 kit counts nine colors [S5] and PaintScratch lists EW3 for 2009 [S509]. Solar Yellow as a 2009 color is [Unverified].
- **2015**: SolarFlare Yellow (EAZ) is on ImportArchive's 2015 list [S20]; the June 2014 press kit names seven colors without it [S11]; PaintScratch lists EAZ as 'Beacon Yellow Tricoat' [S515]. Probably a late addition [Unverified].
- Nissan's parts store sells codes under one name across models, so A15, BW9 and C43 appear there as Sonoma Sunset, Majestic Blue and Greige [S22], while Xterra brochures call them Red Brawn, Midnight Blue and Desert Stone [S20].
- PaintScratch uses paint-supplier names (for example 'Cloud White' for QM1, 'Silver Mist Metallic' for K12) and also lists G64 'Dark Gray (matt)' and LK95 'Graphite Metallic' for most years [S505][S515]. Neither is a body color on ImportArchive's list [S20]; G64 may be the unpainted trim color [Unverified].
- Paint codes here are for US-market vehicles [S20].

## Finding your code

The color code is printed on a label on the vehicle, normally in the driver's door jamb but sometimes under the hood [S505]. Order paint by code, not by name.
