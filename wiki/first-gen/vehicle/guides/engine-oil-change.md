# Engine oil and filter change (KA24DE and VG33E/VG33ER)

[Guides](index.md) · [Reference home](../index.md)

| | |
|---|---|
| Applies to | 2000-2004 Xterra: the KA24DE four-cylinder (2,389 cm³) and the VG33E V6 (3,275 cm³), plus the supercharged VG33ER listed in the 2004 manual [S1][S2][S3][S4] |
| Difficulty | Easy [Unverified] |
| Time | 30-45 minutes [Unverified] |
| Interval | The 2004 manual's severe-use Schedule 1: oil and filter every 3,750 mi (6,000 km) or 3 months. Its normal Schedule 2: every 7,500 mi (12,000 km) or 6 months [S2] |

## Specifications

| Item | KA24DE | VG33E and VG33ER | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Drain and refill with filter, 2000 manual | 3.9 L (4-1/8 US qt) | 3.3 L (3-1/2 US qt) | [S1] |
| Drain and refill without filter, 2000 manual | 3.7 L (3-7/8 US qt) | 3.0 L (3-1/8 US qt) | [S1] |
| Drain and refill with filter, 2004 manual | 3.5 L (3-3/4 US qt) | 3.3 L (3-1/2 US qt) | [S2] |
| Drain and refill without filter, 2004 manual | 3.3 L (3-1/2 US qt) | 3.0 L (3-1/8 US qt) | [S2] |
| Oil pan drain plug, new washer every time | 29-39 N·m (22-29 ft-lb) | 29-39 N·m (22-29 ft-lb) | [S1][S2] |
| Oil filter | Hand-tight until the seal touches, then 2/3 turn more | Same | [S1][S2] |

Nissan's two manuals give different KA24DE capacities. Use the one for your year's manual if you have it, and always finish with the dipstick: the amount depends on oil temperature and drain time [S1][S2].

**Oil:**
- It must carry the API Certification Mark and be API grade SG/SH Energy Conserving I & II or SJ Energy Conserving. The 2004 manual adds SL, and ILSAC GF-III alongside GF-I and GF-II [S1][S2].
- SAE 5W-30 is preferred at all temperatures. 10W-30 or 10W-40 may be used above -18°C (0°F) [S1][S2].

**Filter:** a full-flow cartridge with a built-in relief valve [S1][S2]. For the VG engine, the 2000 manual lists Nissan 15208-31U00. The 2004 manual lists 15208-31U00, 15208-9E000 or 15208-7B000, or an equivalent [S1][S2].

## Tools

- Wrench or socket for the drain plug [Unverified]
- Oil filter wrench. Nissan's is KV10115801 (J-38956) [S2]
- Drain pan, rags, and a new drain plug washer [S1][S2]

## Procedure

**Hot oil burns, and used oil is harmful to skin with repeated contact.** Wash it off with soap or hand cleaner as soon as you can [S1][S2].

1. Warm up the engine and look for oil leaks [S1][S2].
2. Stop the engine. On the VG33E and VG33ER, Nissan says to wait more than 10 minutes before draining [S1][S2].
3. Remove the drain plug and the oil filler cap, and let the oil drain [S1][S2].
4. Clean the drain plug, fit a **new washer**, and torque it to **29-39 N·m (22-29 ft-lb)** [S1][S2].
5. Remove the filter with a filter wrench [S1][S2].
6. Clean the filter mounting surface on the block, and coat the new filter's rubber seal with engine oil [S1][S2].
7. Screw the filter in until you feel slight resistance, then tighten it a further **2/3 turn** [S1][S2].
8. Fill with new oil to the capacity above, and wipe off any spilled oil [S1][S2].
9. Start the engine and check around the drain plug and filter for leaks [S1][S2].
10. Stop the engine, wait several minutes (more than 10 minutes on the V6), and check the level on the dipstick [S1][S2].

## Oil leaks at the back of the pan

One owner of a 2004 lost several starters to oil leaking from the rear of the oil pan. On his 4WD, resealing the pan meant first removing the front differential and unbolting the front crossmember mounts. The pan bolts are all 10 mm; the bolts at the two rubber mounts at the back are different lengths from the rest, so note which came from where [S5].
