# Xterra how-to guides

[Reference home](../index.md) · [First-gen wiki index](../../index.md)

Step-by-step maintenance and repair guides for the first-generation Xterra only: model years 2000-2004 [S1][S2], chassis code WD22 [Unverified]. They are written by hand from Nissan's factory service manuals for these years, read from the Internet Archive's copies. Every step and number cites its source on the guide's own page, and anything no source states is marked [Unverified].

Torque, capacity and part-number values drive real repairs. Where Nissan changed a value between model years, the guide gives each manual year's value. When the guides and your own year's manual disagree, the manual wins.

## How these guides are kept honest

- `xw export` never rewrites this folder; it links each guide from its topic's row in the section's [wiki index](../../index.md).
- `xw qa` checks every guide: a line that states a number must cite a source listed at the bottom of its page, or say [Unverified], and no line may name the other generation. That checks traceability, not truth.
- Factory service manual torque values are printed on the manual's exploded-view drawings; each guide names the manual year, section and page.
