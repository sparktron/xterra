# Supercharger (VG33ER): inspection, belt, rebuild and swaps

[Guides](index.md) · [Reference home](../index.md) · Related: [Timing belt](timing-belt-water-pump.md), [Recalls](../recalls.md)

The supercharged V6 is the VG33ER. In Nissan's model code its fuel system letter is R, "multiport fuel injection system and supercharger", against E for the non-supercharged engine [S1]. It shares the VG33E's timing belt procedure [S2].

**Nissan's rule: do not disassemble or adjust the supercharger.** The factory procedure removes, inspects and refits it as a unit [S2].

## Removal (Nissan's 2004 procedure)

1. Disconnect the negative battery cable [S2].
2. Unhook the accelerator cable, and the cruise control cable if fitted, from the throttle body and the air inlet tube bracket [S2].
3. Remove the air inlet duct, unplugging the PCV and resonator hoses [S2].
4. Drain some of the coolant [S2].
5. Remove the supercharger pulley cover and the supercharger/A/C drive belt [S2].
6. Remove the air inlet tube's upper and lower supports, then its bolts, nuts and studs, and move the tube aside. Unplug the EVAP vacuum hose, the brake booster vacuum hose and both throttle position sensor connectors [S2].
7. Unplug the boost control valve vacuum hose and the PCV hose, then remove the supercharger bolts and the supercharger [S2].

## Inspection

- **Flange:** clean the mating surface and check it against the 0.12 mm (0.005 in) flatness limit with a straightedge and feeler gauge [S2].
- **Rotors:** the pulley should turn smoothly clockwise by hand, needing no more than 0.5 N·m (4 in-lb). Both rotors must be free of cracks and contamination [S2].
- **Bypass valve actuator:** with less than 12 kPa (3.54 inHg) applied at the lower hose port it must not leak. The rod should start to move at about 12 kPa and be fully out at about 33.3 kPa (9.84 inHg) [S2].

## Installation

Refit in the reverse order. Use new gaskets on clean, flat, undamaged faces, tighten each part in its numbered sequence to the torques above, and refill the coolant [S2].

## Fuel

The 2004 manual has two fuel precautions: unleaded regular of at least 87 AKI, and unleaded premium of at least 91 AKI "required", where regular may be used only temporarily (partial fill, no full-throttle driving) [S1]. That section doesn't say which engine each applies to [S1]. One owner says to run 92+ octane on a supercharger swap that lacks the boost control solenoid [S5].

**Recall:** on 2002 supercharged trucks, a cold engine at full throttle in a low gear could push the engine control into fail-safe, limiting it to 2,400 rpm. Dealers reprogrammed the engine control module (campaign 02V298000) [S4]. See [Recalls](../recalls.md).

## What owners add

- **Rebuilds:** the blower is a roots type, so it has to come off the engine to be rebuilt from underneath [S6].
  - One owner's blower began to sound like marbles. He found rebuild kits that include bearings and oil [S6].
  - Another found only the coupler had failed, and replaced it [S6].
  - A third rebuilt his donor blower with a kit, replacing the coupler, oil and needle bearings. He read that the Cobalt SS blower uses the same internal parts [S5].
  - Drain the old blower oil outdoors: one owner calls it the worst-smelling substance he has met [S5].
- **More boost:** owners fit a smaller pulley, which spins the blower faster [S6].
- **Swapping a supercharger onto a non-supercharged VG33E:** one owner's list [S5]:
  - **Parts:**
    - throttle body and cable;
    - brake booster line;
    - intake tube and its hoses;
    - the blower with its bypass valve;
    - intake adapter and intake manifold;
    - fuel rail with its injectors;
    - boost control solenoid;
    - pulley bridge, A/C compressor, harmonic balancer and ECM.
  - **PCV:** on the supercharged engine it sits on the passenger-side valve cover, not the intake plenum [S5].
  - **Boost control solenoid:** optional, but without it the supercharged ECM sets a check engine light [S5].
  - **Pulleys:** use the supercharged engine's 6-rib balancer and A/C compressor pulley, because the stock pulleys reportedly differ in size [S5].
  - **ECM:** he recommends the supercharged ECM. The non-supercharged one reportedly runs very rich at idle [S5].
  - **Bolts:** one intake bolt nearly broke during his install. Tighten in three stages (finger tight, half spec, full spec) [S5].
  - **Distributor:** if you pull it, set the engine at TDC and mark it first [S5].
