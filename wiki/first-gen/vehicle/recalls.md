# Recalls (2000-2004)

[Reference home](index.md)

Safety recalls that NHTSA lists for the 2000-2004 Nissan Xterra [S1][S5], newest first within each group. Check your own VIN on NHTSA's or Nissan's recall lookup: several of these recalls cover only part of a model year's production.

## Nissan recalls

| Campaign | Model years | Component | What happened and the remedy | Filed | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 10V554000 | 2002-2004 | Lower steering column joint | Trucks built July 9, 2001 to January 6, 2005: corrosion can stiffen the lower steering column joint, overloading the shaft, which can crack during low-speed maneuvers with large steering inputs. Dealers replace the steering shaft and joint with a different design, free. The recall began December 13, 2010 | 2010-11-11 | [S3][S4][S5] |
| 04V581000 | 2004 | Accessory tow hitch | The accessory tow hitch's attachment bolts may not have been tightened to specification, so the hitch could loosen or separate. Dealers tighten the hitch. The recall began January 3, 2005 | 2004-12-15 | [S5] |
| 04V230000 | 2000-2003 | Fuel pump terminal on the fuel sending unit | The plastic molding around the fuel pump terminal can crack, letting the terminal strip corrode and eventually break, so the pump stops: the engine won't start or stalls without warning. In 22 listed states and Washington, DC, dealers replace the sending unit; elsewhere they inspect the terminal for corrosion | 2004-05-24 | [S1][S2][S3][S4] |
| 03V307000 | 2000 | Alloy wheels | 2000 Xterras built October 15, 1999 to February 29, 2000: the alloy wheels may not have been made properly and could crack in the spokes. Nissan replaces the four alloy wheels with SE bright alloy wheels, free | 2003-08-22 | [S1] |
| 03V061000 | 2002 | Driver air bag connector | The driver air bag's electrical connector may come loose, making the air bag warning light flash intermittently. Dealers fit a retaining clip on the clock spring connector | 2003-02-27 | [S3] |
| 02V300000 | 2001 | Fuel tank inlet shutter valve | The fuel tank inlet shutter valve does not meet its sealing specification. Dealers replace it | 2002-11-12 | [S2] |
| 02V299000 | 2001-2003 | Rear axle (ABS sensor seal) | Water can get into the rear axle housing past a poorly sealed ABS sensor. Dealers inspect the axle for water and replace affected parts, then reseal the sensor | 2002-11-12 | [S2][S3][S4] |
| 02V298000 | 2002 | Engine control (supercharged engine) | On supercharged trucks, full throttle in a low gear on a cold engine in cold weather can push the air flow meter reading past its diagnostic limit. The engine control then goes into fail-safe: the check engine light comes on and the engine won't rev past 2,400 rpm. Dealers reprogram the engine control module | 2002-11-12 | [S3] |
| 00V288000 | 2000 | Automatic transmission shift cable lock plate | The shift cable lock plate can be brittle and break. The lever and PRNDL indicator then move, but the transmission stays in Park, and it can later shift into a gear other than the one shown. Dealers replace the lock plate | 2000-10-02 | [S1] |
