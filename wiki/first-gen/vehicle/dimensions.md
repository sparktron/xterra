# Dimensions

[Reference home](index.md)

Nissan published no first-generation figures that this page has checked yet. The numbers below come from the 2005 press kit, which compares the redesigned 2005 Xterra with the outgoing model [S1]. The kit does not say which model year of the outgoing Xterra it measured [Unverified].

## Exterior

| Measure | Outgoing Xterra | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Overall width | 70.4 in | [S1] |
| Overall height | 74.4 in | [S1] |

The 2005 kit gives the outgoing model's wheelbase as 2.0 in shorter and its overall length as 0.2 in shorter than the 2005 model's [S1].

## Interior

| Measure | Outgoing Xterra | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Front leg room | 41.4 in | [S1] |
| Front head room | 38.6 in | [S1] |

Against the 2005 model, the outgoing model's second row had 1.6 in less leg room, 1.5 in less head room and 3.9 in less shoulder room [S1].

- [S1] Nissan North America, 2005 Nissan Xterra Press Kit (overview, at-a-glance). https://usa.nissannews.com/en-US/releases/release-f82282403bb549d28fdb180ec46bd9d5-2005-nissan-xterra-press-kit-3
